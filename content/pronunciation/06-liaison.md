Most final consonants are silent (petit, grand, vous), except often c, r, f and l, the consonants of “careful”. Before a word starting with a vowel or a silent h, a silent consonant can link up with it: s and x sound like z, d sounds like t, so les amis sounds like “lé-za-mi”.

## Sentences

- Les enfants sont avec leurs amis. | The children are with their friends.
- Vous avez deux heures pour finir. | You have two hours to finish.
- Je vous présente mon petit ami, Paul. | Let me introduce my boyfriend, Paul.
- Nous allons dormir dans un grand hôtel. | We're going to sleep in a big hotel.
- Ils ont un chien et deux chats. | They have a dog and two cats.
- Il est huit heures et demie. | It's half past eight.
- Mon ami habite aux États-Unis. | My friend lives in the United States.
- Les petits oiseaux chantent dehors. | The little birds are singing outside.
