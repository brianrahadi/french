id: eu
title: eu and œu
sound: eu · œu
---

Round your lips as if to say “o” while your tongue says “é”: that gives the closed eu of deux and bleu. For the eu of peur and sœur, open your mouth a little more, close to the vowel in “her” (without the r) but with rounded lips.

## Sentences

- J'ai deux sœurs et un neveu. | I have two sisters and a nephew.
- Ma sœur a peur du feu. | My sister is afraid of fire.
- Il pleut depuis deux heures. | It's been raining for two hours.
- Je veux un œuf et du beurre. | I want an egg and some butter.
- Leur fils a les yeux bleus. | Their son has blue eyes.
- Le professeur est très heureux. | The teacher is very happy.
- On se voit vers neuf heures ? | Shall we meet around nine?
- Je peux venir un peu plus tard ? | Can I come a bit later?
