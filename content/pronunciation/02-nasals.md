---
id: nasals
title: Nasal vowels
sound: an/en · on · in/ain/un
---

Let the air flow through your nose and mouth at the same time, and don't pronounce the n or m at all. For an/en open your mouth wide as in “father”, for on round your lips into a small “o”, and for in/ain/un spread your lips as in “bat”.

## Sentences

- Mon enfant mange du pain blanc. | My child eats white bread.
- On prend un bon vin blanc ? | Shall we have a nice white wine?
- Ils vont au jardin le lundi matin. | They go to the garden on Monday mornings.
- Le chien de mon voisin est très content. | My neighbour's dog is very happy.
- Vincent chante une chanson en allemand. | Vincent is singing a song in German.
- Nous rentrons demain matin en train. | We're coming back tomorrow morning by train.
- Mon cousin a cinq ans maintenant. | My cousin is five now.
