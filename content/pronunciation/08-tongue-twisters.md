title: Tongue twisters
sound: r · u · ou · eu · an · in
---

Say each sentence slowly first, word by word, then speed up little by little while keeping it smooth. Watch your lips in a mirror: they stay rounded for u, ou and eu, and it's your tongue that makes the difference.

## Sentences

- Il pleut sur la route du retour. | It's raining on the way back.
- Ton oncle arrive vendredi en train. | Your uncle is arriving on Friday by train.
- Ma sœur trouve que ce jus est trop sucré. | My sister thinks this juice is too sweet.
- Je voudrais une grande tarte aux fruits rouges. | I'd like a large red berry tart.
- Deux cents euros ? C'est un peu cher ! | Two hundred euros? That's a bit expensive!
- Tout le monde a bu du vin rouge. | Everyone drank red wine.
- Un écureuil grimpe à l'arbre du jardin. | A squirrel is climbing the tree in the garden.
- Heureusement, le serrurier arrive tout de suite. | Luckily, the locksmith is coming straight away.
