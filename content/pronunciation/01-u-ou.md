id: u-ou
title: u vs ou
sound: u · ou
---

For ou, round your lips tightly and pull your tongue back, as in “food”. For u, keep exactly the same rounded lips but press the tip of your tongue against your bottom teeth, as if you were saying “ee” while whistling.

## Sentences

- Tu as tout vu ? | Did you see everything?
- Vous avez vu la roue du vélo dans la rue ? | Did you see the bike wheel in the street?
- Il habite tout au bout de la rue. | He lives right at the end of the street.
- Jules joue souvent du tuba. | Jules often plays the tuba.
- Tu as bu tout le jus de pomme ? | Did you drink all the apple juice?
- Où sont les douze tulipes rouges ? | Where are the twelve red tulips?
- Bonjour, vous voulez du sucre ? | Hello, would you like some sugar?
- Lucie court tous les jours sur la plage. | Lucie runs on the beach every day.
