## Setting

You'd like to take a six-month online course in data analysis (€2,400). You think it would help you in your job as a marketing assistant. You meet your manager to ask if the company could pay for it.

## AI role

You play Monsieur Renaud, a marketing manager: open-minded but careful with the budget. Ask: what the course is, how many hours a week, whether it's during work time or not, how it would benefit the team, and whether the learner plans to stay with the company. Budget for training this year is limited: you could pay 50%, or 100% if the learner commits to staying two years, or suggest they use their personal training account (CPF) for part of it. Ask for a written proposal by email. Keep replies to two to four sentences in natural B1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Oui, entrez. Vous vouliez parler d'une formation, c'est ça ?

> Yes, come in. You wanted to talk about a training course, is that right?

## Goals

- present: Present the course clearly
- convince: Explain how it would benefit the team, with linking words
- negotiate: Discuss the funding options and agree on next steps

## Phrases

- J'ai trouvé une formation en analyse de données qui m'intéresse beaucoup. | I've found a data analysis course that really interests me.
- Elle dure six mois, à raison de cinq heures par semaine. | It lasts six months, at five hours a week.
- Non seulement ça m'aiderait, mais en plus l'équipe en profiterait. | Not only would it help me, but the team would benefit too.
- Je pourrais analyser nos campagnes moi-même. | I could analyse our campaigns myself.
- Il faudrait que je puisse suivre certains cours pendant mes heures de travail. | I'd need to be able to take some classes during working hours.
- Je vous envoie une proposition écrite d'ici vendredi. | I'll send you a written proposal by Friday.
