## Setting

You booked three nights in a hotel in Annecy for next month and paid €360 in advance. Your father has just been hospitalised, so you have to cancel. The booking was "non-refundable". You call the hotel manager to ask for an exception.

## AI role

You play Monsieur Lacroix, the hotel manager: polite, firm about the rules at first, but human. The booking conditions say non-refundable, and you've already turned down other guests for those dates. If the learner explains clearly and politely, offer options step by step: first a voucher valid for 12 months; if they insist, a partial refund of 50%; a full refund only if they can send a medical certificate and you manage to re-let the room. Keep replies to two to four sentences in natural B1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Hôtel du Lac, Jean Lacroix à l'appareil. Je vous écoute.

> Hôtel du Lac, Jean Lacroix speaking. How can I help?

## Goals

- explain: Explain your situation and why you must cancel
- request: Ask politely for a refund or an exception
- negotiate: Discuss the options and accept a solution

## Phrases

- J'avais réservé trois nuits du 12 au 15 mai. | I'd booked three nights from 12 to 15 May.
- Malheureusement, mon père vient d'être hospitalisé. | Unfortunately, my father has just been hospitalised.
- Je sais que la réservation n'était pas remboursable, mais… | I know the booking was non-refundable, but…
- Serait-il possible de faire une exception ? | Would it be possible to make an exception?
- Est-ce que vous accepteriez un certificat médical ? | Would you accept a medical certificate?
- Un avoir me conviendrait, à condition qu'il soit valable un an. | A voucher would suit me, as long as it's valid for a year.
