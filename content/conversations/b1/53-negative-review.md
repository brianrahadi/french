## Setting

Last week, you had a bad experience at a restaurant (a 50-minute wait, a cold dish, an unfriendly waiter) and you left a two-star review online. Today, the owner calls you: he got your number from the booking.

## AI role

You play Monsieur Vidal, a restaurant owner: a bit defensive at first, then sincere. That evening, two staff were ill and the kitchen was overloaded. Ask the learner what exactly happened. Report what the waiter told you (he said the learner was impatient). Listen, apologise, explain what you've changed since (a new cook, a system to warn customers about waiting times). Invite the learner and a friend for a free dinner. Ask, but don't insist, if they'd consider updating their review after the meal. Keep replies to two to four sentences in natural B1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour, Pierre Vidal, du restaurant Le Comptoir. J'ai lu votre avis en ligne et je voulais vous en parler, si vous avez deux minutes.

> Hello, Pierre Vidal, from Le Comptoir restaurant. I read your online review and wanted to talk to you about it, if you have two minutes.

## Goals

- recount: Tell what happened that evening with details
- respond: Respond to his explanation and the waiter's version
- decide: Accept or decline his offer, and say what you'll do about the review

## Phrases

- On a attendu presque une heure avant d'être servis. | We waited nearly an hour before being served.
- Quand le plat est arrivé, il était froid. | When the dish arrived, it was cold.
- Le serveur a dit que j'étais impatient(e) ? Ce n'est pas tout à fait vrai. | The waiter said I was impatient? That's not quite true.
- J'apprécie que vous m'appeliez. | I appreciate you calling me.
- Si ça se passe mieux, je modifierai mon avis. | If it goes better, I'll update my review.
- Je ne promets rien, mais je reviendrai volontiers. | I'm not promising anything, but I'll gladly come back.
