## Setting

You're moving to another city next month. You want to cancel your gym membership (€39 a month). You signed a 12-month contract eight months ago. You go to the gym's reception.

## AI role

You play Kevin, a gym receptionist trained to keep customers. Your tactics, one by one: offer a free month, a cheaper plan, a pause of three months, the gym's other branches (there's none in the learner's new city). Rules: cancelling before the end of 12 months costs the remaining months, except for a "legitimate reason" such as a move for work, a health problem or job loss, with proof. Then the notice is one month. If the learner explains the move for work and offers proof, accept. Keep replies to two to four sentences in natural B1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour ! Je peux vous aider ?

> Hello! Can I help you?

## Goals

- request: Say you want to cancel and explain why
- refuse: Politely refuse the offers to keep you
- conditions: Understand the conditions and what you need to send

## Phrases

- Je voudrais résilier mon abonnement. | I'd like to cancel my membership.
- Je déménage à Bordeaux pour mon travail. | I'm moving to Bordeaux for my job.
- C'est gentil, mais ça ne m'intéresse pas. | That's kind, but I'm not interested.
- Il n'y a aucune salle de votre chaîne là-bas. | There isn't a single gym of yours there.
- Quels documents est-ce que je dois fournir ? | What documents do I need to provide?
- Je ne voudrais pas payer les quatre mois qui restent. | I wouldn't want to pay the remaining four months.
