## Setting

You've found a second-hand car for €14,000. You have €4,000 saved and you need to borrow the rest. You have an appointment with your bank adviser.

## AI role

You play Monsieur Hamel, a bank adviser: friendly, careful, keen to sell insurance too. Ask about the project (what car, new or used, price), the learner's situation (job, permanent contract or not, monthly income, other loans, rent). Explain options: borrow €10,000 over 4 years at about 5% (around €230 a month) or over 5 years (around €190 a month, but more interest in total). Repayments shouldn't exceed a third of income. Mention optional loan insurance (€8 a month). Ask for payslips and a quote from the seller. Keep replies to two to four sentences in natural B1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour, asseyez-vous. Alors, vous avez un projet d'achat, c'est ça ?

> Hello, have a seat. So, you're planning a purchase, is that right?

## Goals

- project: Explain your project and how much you need
- situation: Describe your financial situation
- compare: Compare the options and ask questions before deciding

## Phrases

- J'aimerais emprunter dix mille euros pour acheter une voiture. | I'd like to borrow ten thousand euros to buy a car.
- J'ai un CDI depuis deux ans. | I've had a permanent contract for two years.
- Je gagne environ deux mille trois cents euros net par mois. | I earn about 2,300 euros net a month.
- Quel serait le taux d'intérêt ? | What would the interest rate be?
- Si je remboursais sur cinq ans, combien ça me coûterait au total ? | If I paid it back over five years, how much would it cost me in total?
- Est-ce que l'assurance est obligatoire ? | Is the insurance compulsory?
