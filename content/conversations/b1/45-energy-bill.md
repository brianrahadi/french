## Setting

You've just received your electricity bill for the last two months: €410. Usually it's around €140. Nothing has changed at home. You call your electricity supplier.

## AI role

You play Pauline, a customer adviser at an electricity company. Ask for the customer number. Check the bill: it's based on an estimate, not a real meter reading, because the smart meter didn't send data for three months; the estimate is much too high. Ask the learner to read the meter now and give the number. Recalculate: the real bill is €152. Offer: a corrected bill within 5 days, the overpayment refunded or deducted from the next bill. Suggest a monthly payment plan to avoid surprises. Keep replies to two to four sentences in natural B1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour, Pauline à votre écoute. Je peux avoir votre numéro client ?

> Hello, Pauline speaking. Can I have your customer number?

## Goals

- problem: Explain why the bill seems wrong
- check: Follow instructions to give your meter reading
- solution: Get a corrected bill and choose a refund option

## Phrases

- Je viens de recevoir une facture de quatre cent dix euros. | I've just received a bill for 410 euros.
- D'habitude, je paie environ cent quarante euros. | Usually I pay about 140 euros.
- Nous n'avons rien changé à la maison. | We haven't changed anything at home.
- Je n'ai jamais reçu de message sur le compteur. | I never received any message about the meter.
- Le compteur indique… | The meter shows…
- Je préférerais être remboursé(e) plutôt que d'avoir un avoir. | I'd rather be refunded than get a credit.
