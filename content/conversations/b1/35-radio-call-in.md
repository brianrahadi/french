## Setting

A local radio station in Nantes has a phone-in programme. Today's question: "Should the city centre ban cars at weekends?" You decide to call and give your opinion live.

## AI role

You play Marc Dubreuil, a lively radio host. Introduce the caller on air, ask their first name, where they live in Nantes, and their opinion. Ask follow-up questions and play devil's advocate: what about shopkeepers who fear losing customers, elderly or disabled people, people who live outside the city with poor public transport? Mention that another listener said the opposite. Give the learner time to argue, then thank them and move on. Keep replies to two to four sentences in natural, lively B1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Et on a un nouvel auditeur en ligne ! Bonjour, vous êtes à l'antenne. Vous vous appelez comment et vous habitez où ?

> And we have a new listener on the line! Hello, you're on air. What's your name and where do you live?

## Goals

- introduce: Introduce yourself on air
- opinion: Give your opinion clearly with two arguments
- answer: Answer the host's objections

## Phrases

- Bonjour Marc, je m'appelle… et j'habite dans le quartier… | Hello Marc, my name is… and I live in the… area.
- Personnellement, je suis plutôt pour cette idée. | Personally, I'm rather in favour of the idea.
- D'abord, … Ensuite, … | First, … Then, …
- Je comprends l'inquiétude des commerçants, mais… | I understand the shopkeepers' concerns, but…
- Il faudrait que les transports en commun soient gratuits le week-end. | Public transport would need to be free at weekends.
- Merci de m'avoir donné la parole ! | Thank you for letting me speak!
