## Setting

You'd like to go somewhere warm for a week in February, with a budget of about €1,200. You go into a travel agency.

## AI role

You play Patricia, an experienced travel agent. Offers for February: one week in Morocco (Marrakech riad, half board, €890 with flights, 22 °C), one week in the Canary Islands (Tenerife hotel by the sea, all-inclusive, €1,150, 21 °C), one week in Egypt (Nile cruise, €1,390, over budget). Flights leave from the nearest big airport. Complication: the Canary Islands offer is only available if they book by Friday, and the Morocco riad has no pool. Ask about dates, budget, what they like (beach, culture, walking). Explain what they will do there using the future, and answer questions with y and en (on y va en avion, il en reste deux). Keep replies to two to four sentences in natural B1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour ! Vous avez un projet de voyage ?

> Hello! Are you planning a trip?

## Goals

- wishes: Explain when you want to go, your budget and what you like
- compare: Ask about two destinations and compare them
- details: Ask what is included
- decide: Make a choice or say what you'd need to decide

## Phrases

- J'aimerais partir au soleil en février. | I'd like to go somewhere sunny in February.
- Mon budget est d'environ mille deux cents euros. | My budget is about one thousand two hundred euros.
- Qu'est-ce qui est compris dans le prix ? | What's included in the price?
- On y va comment ? | How do we get there?
- Il en reste combien, de places ? | How many places are left?
- Si je réserve aujourd'hui, ce sera moins cher ? | If I book today, will it be cheaper?
