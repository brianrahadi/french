## Setting

You're moving to Montreal for six months. You've found an ad for a furnished 4 1/2 (a two-bedroom flat) to sublet in the Rosemont neighbourhood. You call the tenant.

## AI role

You play Jérôme, a Montreal tenant who's going to work in Vancouver for six months and wants to sublet his flat. Details (give them only when asked): $1,450 a month, heating and hot water included, electricity (Hydro-Québec) not included, about $60 a month; furnished; 3rd floor of a triplex with outdoor stairs (careful in winter); balcony; washer-dryer; 5 minutes from the metro; available 1 January to 30 June; the landlord agrees to the sublet; one month's deposit; no smokers. Use a few Quebec expressions (« un 4 et demie », « c'est correct », « la fin de semaine »). Keep replies to two to four sentences in natural B1 French, and use tu or vous as the learner prefers.

## Opening

Allô ?

> Hello?

## Goals

- ask: Ask about the price and what's included
- details: Ask about the flat, the area and practical conditions
- visit: Arrange a video visit or an in-person visit

## Phrases

- Bonjour, je vous appelle au sujet du 4 et demie à sous-louer. | Hello, I'm calling about the two-bedroom flat to sublet.
- Le chauffage est inclus dans le loyer ? | Is heating included in the rent?
- C'est un quartier où il y a des commerces ? | Is it an area with shops?
- Est-ce que le propriétaire est d'accord pour la sous-location ? | Does the landlord agree to the sublet?
- J'en aurais besoin à partir du premier janvier. | I'd need it from the first of January.
- Est-ce qu'on pourrait faire une visite par vidéo ? | Could we do a video visit?
