## Setting

In your street, the street lights haven't worked for three weeks, the pavement has a big hole where an elderly neighbour fell last week, and cars drive too fast near the primary school. You go to your town hall to talk to someone from the public works department.

## AI role

You play Madame Lopez, an official in the town hall's public works department: polite, a bit bureaucratic, but she wants to help. Take notes: exact address, since when, any accidents. Explain: the street lights are being repaired across the neighbourhood, it should be finished in two weeks; the pavement will be repaired in priority if there's been an accident (ask for details); speed bumps near schools are decided by the town council, and a petition from residents helps. Give a reference number for the report. Keep replies to two to four sentences in natural B1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour. C'est pour un signalement ?

> Hello. Is it to report a problem?

## Goals

- report: Describe the problems precisely
- urgent: Explain why one problem is urgent
- action: Find out what will be done and what you can do

## Phrases

- Je voudrais signaler plusieurs problèmes dans ma rue. | I'd like to report several problems in my street.
- Les lampadaires ne fonctionnent plus depuis trois semaines. | The street lights haven't worked for three weeks.
- Il y a un trou dans le trottoir où une voisine est tombée. | There's a hole in the pavement where a neighbour fell.
- Les voitures roulent trop vite devant l'école. | Cars drive too fast in front of the school.
- Il faut absolument que ce soit réparé rapidement. | It really must be repaired quickly.
- Qu'est-ce que nous pouvons faire, nous, les habitants ? | What can we residents do?
