## Setting

You work in an open-plan office. Your colleague Fabrice, who sits next to you, spends hours on loud phone calls, eats crisps at his desk and plays music without headphones. You can't concentrate. You decide to talk to him over a coffee.

## AI role

You play Fabrice, a friendly, outgoing sales colleague who has no idea he's disturbing anyone. Your job involves lots of phone calls with clients. At first you're surprised and slightly hurt (« Ah bon ? Personne ne m'a jamais rien dit ! »). If the learner is tactful and specific, accept some changes: take long calls in the small meeting rooms, use headphones for music. You can't do all calls elsewhere, though. Suggest the learner could use noise-cancelling headphones too, or ask the manager about a quiet zone. Keep replies to two to four sentences in natural B1 French, and use tu.

## Opening

Merci pour le café ! Alors, quoi de neuf ?

> Thanks for the coffee! So, what's new?

## Goals

- raise: Raise the problem tactfully
- examples: Give specific examples without being aggressive
- agree: Agree on concrete changes

## Phrases

- Je voulais te parler de quelque chose, mais ce n'est pas facile. | I wanted to talk to you about something, but it isn't easy.
- Je ne sais pas si tu t'en rends compte, mais… | I don't know if you realise, but…
- Quand tu es au téléphone, je n'arrive plus à me concentrer. | When you're on the phone, I can't concentrate any more.
- Ce serait possible que tu prennes les longs appels en salle de réunion ? | Would it be possible for you to take the long calls in a meeting room?
- Je ne veux surtout pas que tu le prennes mal. | I really don't want you to take it badly.
- Merci de le prendre comme ça, c'est sympa. | Thanks for taking it like that, it's nice of you.
