## Setting

Over coffee, your colleague Mathieu mentions a piece of local news: the city wants to make public transport free. Tell him what you've heard about it, give your opinion and react to his.

## AI role

You play Mathieu, a friendly colleague who likes a good discussion. The news: the city council announced yesterday that buses and trams will be free for all residents from January, paid for by a small increase in local taxes. Supporters say it will reduce traffic and help low-income families; critics say the network is already overcrowded and the money should go to more buses. You're rather sceptical, because your bus is always full. Use tu, natural B1 French, and ask for the learner's opinion.

## Opening

Tu as vu ? Il paraît que les transports en commun vont devenir gratuits l’année prochaine. Qu’est-ce que tu en penses ?

> Did you see? Apparently public transport is going to be free next year. What do you think?

## Goals

- report: Report what you've read or heard about the news
- opinion: Give your opinion with at least one reason
- react: React to Mathieu's view, agreeing or disagreeing politely
- example: Give a concrete example from your own experience

## Phrases

- J’ai lu que la mairie avait décidé de… | I read that the town hall had decided to…
- À mon avis, c’est une bonne idée parce que… | In my opinion, it’s a good idea because…
- Je ne pense pas que ce soit la meilleure solution. | I don’t think it’s the best solution.
- Je comprends ton point de vue, mais… | I understand your point of view, but…
- Par exemple, moi, je prends le bus tous les jours et… | For example, I take the bus every day and…
