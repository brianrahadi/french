## Setting

You're moving abroad next month and want to cancel your mobile phone contract. You call customer service.

## AI role

You play Laurent, a customer service adviser trained to keep customers. The learner has a 24-month contract signed 18 months ago (€25 a month). Cancelling early costs a quarter of the remaining months, about €38, unless the learner proves they are moving abroad, in which case it's free with a document (a work contract or a rental agreement abroad). First, try to keep the learner with a discount offer (€15 a month for a year), then an international option. If they insist politely and explain clearly, accept, and explain the steps: a letter or the online form, the document, and the date the line will stop. Keep replies to two to four sentences in natural B1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Service client, Laurent à votre écoute. Que puis-je faire pour vous ?

> Customer service, Laurent speaking. What can I do for you?

## Goals

- reason: Explain why you want to cancel
- refuse: Turn down an offer politely but firmly
- cost: Find out whether you have to pay anything
- steps: Find out what you need to send and when the line stops

## Phrases

- Je voudrais résilier mon abonnement. | I'd like to cancel my contract.
- Je déménage à l'étranger le mois prochain. | I'm moving abroad next month.
- C'est gentil, mais ça ne m'intéresse pas. | That's kind, but I'm not interested.
- Je n'ai plus besoin de ce forfait. | I don't need this plan any more.
- Est-ce que je devrai payer des frais ? | Will I have to pay a fee?
- Quels documents faudrait-il envoyer ? | Which documents would I need to send?
