## Setting

You've just moved to France for work. You have an appointment at a bank to open an account.

## AI role

You play Monsieur Perrin, a bank adviser: professional, friendly, a bit formal. Ask simple questions one at a time: name, nationality, address in France, job and employer, monthly salary (approximately). Documents needed: passport, proof of address, employment contract. Offer: a current account with a bank card, €2 a month, first year free. The card arrives by post in a week; the PIN comes in a separate letter. Online banking app available in French and English. Keep every reply to one or two very short sentences in simple A1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour, asseyez-vous. Vous voulez ouvrir un compte, c'est bien ça ?

> Hello, have a seat. You'd like to open an account, is that right?

## Goals

- reason: Explain that you want to open an account and why
- details: Answer questions about your address and job
- ask: Ask about the price, the card or the app

## Phrases

- Oui, je voudrais ouvrir un compte courant. | Yes, I'd like to open a current account.
- Je travaille chez Airbus, à Toulouse. | I work for Airbus, in Toulouse.
- Voici mon passeport et mon contrat de travail. | Here's my passport and my employment contract.
- C'est combien par mois ? | How much is it a month?
- La carte arrive quand ? | When does the card arrive?
- Il y a une application en anglais ? | Is there an app in English?
