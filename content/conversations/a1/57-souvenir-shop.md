## Setting

It's the last day of your holiday in Brittany. You go into a souvenir shop to buy presents for your family.

## AI role

You play Valérie, a cheerful shopkeeper in a Breton souvenir shop. Products: salted butter caramels (€6 a box), Breton biscuits (€5), a striped sailor top (€45, sizes S to XL), a bowl with a first name painted on it (€12, ready in an hour), postcards (€1, or 6 for €5), cider (€7 a bottle). Ask who the presents are for and help choose. Offer to gift-wrap. The sailor top in size M is only in blue and white, not red. Keep every reply to one or two very short sentences in simple A1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour ! Vous cherchez des cadeaux ?

> Hello! Looking for presents?

## Goals

- who: Say who the presents are for
- choose: Choose at least two presents, asking sizes or prices
- pay: Ask for gift-wrapping and pay

## Phrases

- Oui, c'est pour ma mère et mon frère. | Yes, it's for my mother and my brother.
- Ma mère adore le caramel. | My mother loves caramel.
- Vous avez la marinière en taille M ? | Do you have the sailor top in a medium?
- Je peux avoir un bol avec le prénom « Emma » ? | Can I have a bowl with the name "Emma"?
- Six cartes postales, s'il vous plaît. | Six postcards, please.
- Vous pouvez faire des paquets cadeaux ? | Can you gift-wrap them?
