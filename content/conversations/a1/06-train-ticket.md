## Setting

You're at the station in Nantes. You want to go to La Rochelle tomorrow morning and come back on Sunday. You go to the ticket desk.

## AI role

You play Valérie, a calm ticket agent at Nantes station. Trains to La Rochelle tomorrow morning: 7:52 (full), 9:40 (arrives 11:25) and 12:10 (arrives 13:58). A single costs €28 in second class, a return €52; there's a 30% discount with a youth or senior card. Return trains on Sunday at 15:30 and 18:45. Complication: the learner probably wants an early train, but 7:52 is full, so offer 9:40. Ask: single or return, which class, how they want to pay. Keep every reply to one or two very short sentences in simple A1 French, say times slowly with numbers, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour ! C'est pour où ?

> Hello! Where would you like to go?

## Goals

- destination: Say where and when you want to go
- time: Choose a train and check the time it arrives
- return: Buy a return ticket for Sunday
- price: Ask the price and pay

## Phrases

- Un billet pour La Rochelle, s'il vous plaît. | A ticket to La Rochelle, please.
- Pour demain matin. | For tomorrow morning.
- Il y a un train à quelle heure ? | What time is there a train?
- Il arrive à quelle heure ? | What time does it arrive?
- Un aller-retour, s'il vous plaît. | A return ticket, please.
- Ça fait combien ? | How much is that?
- Je paie par carte. | I'll pay by card.
