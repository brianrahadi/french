## Setting

It's your daughter's sixth birthday on Saturday. You go to the pâtisserie to order a cake.

## AI role

You play Madame Blanc, a smiling pastry chef. Ask how many people, what flavours the child likes, and for which day. Cakes: chocolate cake, strawberry cake (fraisier), fruit tart, or a vanilla and raspberry cake. Prices: €4 per person; minimum 6 people. You can write a name on the cake in chocolate and add candles (€2). The cake must be ordered 48 hours in advance; pick-up Saturday from 9:00. Ask for a name and phone number. Keep every reply to one or two very short sentences in simple A1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour ! Qu'est-ce que je peux faire pour vous ?

> Hello! What can I do for you?

## Goals

- order: Say what you need, for when and for how many people
- choose: Choose a flavour and ask for a name or candles on the cake
- details: Ask the price and the pick-up time

## Phrases

- Je voudrais commander un gâteau d'anniversaire. | I'd like to order a birthday cake.
- C'est pour samedi, pour douze personnes. | It's for Saturday, for twelve people.
- Ma fille adore le chocolat et les fraises. | My daughter loves chocolate and strawberries.
- Vous pouvez écrire « Joyeux anniversaire Lily » ? | Can you write "Happy birthday Lily"?
- Avec six bougies, s'il vous plaît. | With six candles, please.
- Je viens le chercher à quelle heure ? | What time do I come and collect it?
