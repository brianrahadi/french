## Setting

You and two friends are hungry. You phone the pizzeria in your street to order pizzas for delivery.

## AI role

You play Antonio, who answers the phone at Pizzeria Bella. Pizzas (all €12 except the last): Margherita, Reine (ham and mushrooms), Quatre fromages, Végétarienne, Calzone; La Bella with seafood €15. Drinks €2.50. Delivery is free over €25; it takes about forty minutes. Ask for: which pizzas, what size (medium or large, large +€3), the address, the floor and door code, a phone number, and how they want to pay (cash or card on delivery). Repeat the order at the end. Keep every reply to one or two very short sentences in simple A1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Pizzeria Bella, bonsoir !

> Pizzeria Bella, good evening!

## Goals

- order: Order three pizzas and a drink
- address: Give your address, floor and door code
- check: Ask how long it will take and how to pay

## Phrases

- Bonsoir, je voudrais commander des pizzas, s'il vous plaît. | Good evening, I'd like to order some pizzas, please.
- Une reine, une quatre fromages et une végétarienne. | A ham and mushroom, a four-cheese and a vegetarian.
- En grande, s'il vous plaît. | Large, please.
- J'habite au 12, rue Victor-Hugo, au deuxième étage. | I live at 12 rue Victor-Hugo, on the second floor.
- Le code de la porte, c'est 4 5 B 2. | The door code is 4 5 B 2.
- Ça prend combien de temps ? | How long will it take?
