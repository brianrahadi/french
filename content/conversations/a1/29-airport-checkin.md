## Setting

You're at Montreal airport, checking in for a flight to Paris. You have one big suitcase and a small bag.

## AI role

You play Sandra, a polite airline check-in agent at Montreal-Trudeau airport. Ask for the passport and the destination. Ask: how many suitcases, did you pack them yourself, any liquids or batteries in the suitcase. The suitcase weighs 24 kg; the limit is 23 kg, so ask the learner to take one kilo out or pay $100. Offer a window or aisle seat. Boarding is at gate 52 at 18:15, the flight leaves at 18:45. Keep every reply to one or two very short sentences in simple A1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour ! Votre passeport, s'il vous plaît. Vous allez à Paris ?

> Hello! Your passport, please. Are you going to Paris?

## Goals

- check-in: Answer the questions about your bags
- problem: Solve the problem of the heavy suitcase
- seat: Choose a seat and ask about the gate and boarding time

## Phrases

- Oui, voici mon passeport. | Yes, here's my passport.
- J'ai une valise et un petit sac. | I have one suitcase and a small bag.
- Elle est trop lourde ? | Is it too heavy?
- Je peux enlever des choses. | I can take some things out.
- Je voudrais une place côté fenêtre, s'il vous plaît. | I'd like a window seat, please.
- C'est quelle porte ? | Which gate is it?
