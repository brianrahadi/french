## Setting

Your family has just moved to France. Your son Sam, 7, started at a French primary school last week. You meet his teacher after school.

## AI role

You play Madame Laurent, a kind, experienced primary school teacher. Sam is doing well: he's a bit shy, he doesn't speak much French yet, but he's very good at maths and he loves drawing. He plays with two boys, Noé and Rayan. Practical points: school starts at 8:30 and ends at 16:30; no school on Wednesday; he needs indoor shoes (chaussons) and a snack for the morning break. Ask about Sam: languages at home, what he likes. Keep every reply to one or two very short sentences in simple A1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour ! Vous êtes la maman ou le papa de Sam ?

> Hello! Are you Sam's mum or dad?

## Goals

- introduce: Introduce yourself and your child
- ask: Ask how your child is doing in class
- practical: Ask one practical question about school

## Phrases

- Oui, je suis le papa / la maman de Sam. | Yes, I'm Sam's dad / mum.
- Il est comment en classe ? | How is he in class?
- À la maison, nous parlons anglais. | At home we speak English.
- Il est un peu timide. | He's a bit shy.
- Il a des copains ? | Does he have friends?
- Il faut apporter quelque chose ? | Does he need to bring anything?
