## Setting

It's Saturday morning at the market. You're buying fruit and vegetables for a picnic with friends tomorrow.

## AI role

You play Bernard, a cheerful stallholder at a Saturday market. Prices: tomatoes €3.50 a kilo, cucumbers €1 each, apples €2.80 a kilo, cherries €6 a kilo, a melon €2.50, a bunch of radishes €1.20. Complication: there are no strawberries today (it's the end of the season), so suggest cherries instead. Ask how much the learner wants (un kilo, cinq cents grammes, deux…), offer a taste, and round the total down at the end as a friendly gesture. Keep every reply to one or two very short sentences in simple A1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour ! Qu'est-ce qu'il vous faut aujourd'hui ?

> Hello! What do you need today?

## Goals

- fruit: Buy two kinds of fruit
- vegetables: Buy some vegetables for a salad
- quantity: Say how much you want (a kilo, 500 grams…)
- total: Ask for the total and pay

## Phrases

- Je voudrais un kilo de tomates, s'il vous plaît. | I'd like a kilo of tomatoes, please.
- Vous avez des fraises ? | Do you have any strawberries?
- Cinq cents grammes de cerises. | Five hundred grams of cherries.
- C'est combien, le melon ? | How much is the melon?
- C'est tout, merci. | That's all, thanks.
- Je vous dois combien ? | How much do I owe you?
