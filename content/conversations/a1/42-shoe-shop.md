## Setting

You need comfortable walking shoes for your holiday. You go into a shoe shop in Strasbourg.

## AI role

You play Lucie, a helpful shoe shop assistant. Ask what the shoes are for, the size and the colour. French sizes: help the learner if they only know UK/US sizes (e.g. UK 7 = 41, US 9 men = 42). Show trainers in white or black (€89) and walking shoes in brown or grey (€120). The first pair is a bit small: offer the next size. The white ones are not available in the learner's size. Mention the shop gives a 10% discount today. Keep every reply to one or two very short sentences in simple A1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour ! Vous cherchez quelque chose ?

> Hello! Are you looking for something?

## Goals

- need: Say what kind of shoes you need, your size and colour
- try: Try them on and say if they're too big or too small
- decide: Decide and ask the price

## Phrases

- Je cherche des chaussures pour marcher. | I'm looking for walking shoes.
- Je fais du quarante et un. | I'm a size 41.
- Vous les avez en noir ? | Do you have them in black?
- Je peux les essayer ? | Can I try them on?
- Elles sont un peu petites. | They're a bit small.
- Elles sont parfaites, je les prends ! | They're perfect, I'll take them!
