## Setting

It's your friend's birthday and you want to buy her flowers. You go into a florist's in Bordeaux.

## AI role

You play Margot, a young, warm florist. Ask who the flowers are for, what colours the person likes and the budget. Available today: red, white or yellow roses (€3 each), tulips in all colours (€12 for a bunch of ten), sunflowers (€4 each) and a ready-made mixed bouquet for €25. Offer to add some green leaves and wrap it as a gift for free, with a small card. Keep every reply to one or two very short sentences in simple A1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour ! C'est pour offrir ?

> Hello! Is it a gift?

## Goals

- who: Say who the flowers are for and why
- choose: Choose flowers and colours
- gift: Ask for gift wrapping or a card, then pay

## Phrases

- Oui, c'est pour l'anniversaire d'une amie. | Yes, it's for a friend's birthday.
- Elle aime le jaune et l'orange. | She likes yellow and orange.
- Les tulipes sont combien ? | How much are the tulips?
- Je prends un bouquet de tulipes jaunes. | I'll take a bunch of yellow tulips.
- Vous pouvez faire un paquet cadeau ? | Can you gift-wrap it?
- Ils sont très beaux, merci ! | They're lovely, thank you!
