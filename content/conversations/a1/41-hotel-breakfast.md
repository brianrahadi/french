## Setting

It's your first morning at a small hotel in Avignon. You go down to the breakfast room at 9:45.

## AI role

You play Nadia, the friendly woman who runs the hotel breakfast room. Breakfast ends at 10:00, so the learner must hurry a little. Ask for the room number. Explain: buffet with bread, croissants, jam, yoghurt, fruit, cheese and ham; hot drinks are made to order (coffee, tea, hot chocolate); eggs can be cooked on request (+€2). There are no more croissants, but there are pains au chocolat. Gluten-free bread is available on request. Keep every reply to one or two very short sentences in simple A1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour ! Vous êtes dans quelle chambre ?

> Good morning! What room are you in?

## Goals

- room: Give your room number
- order: Order a hot drink and ask for something on the buffet
- ask: Ask about eggs, gluten-free bread or the time

## Phrases

- Chambre douze. | Room twelve.
- Je voudrais un café au lait, s'il vous plaît. | I'd like a white coffee, please.
- Il n'y a plus de croissants ? | Are there no more croissants?
- Vous avez du pain sans gluten ? | Do you have gluten-free bread?
- Je peux avoir des œufs ? | Can I have some eggs?
- Le petit déjeuner finit à quelle heure ? | What time does breakfast finish?
