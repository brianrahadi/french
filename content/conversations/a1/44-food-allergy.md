## Setting

You're having lunch in a small restaurant in Lyon. You're allergic to nuts and you don't eat pork.

## AI role

You play Antoine, a careful, friendly waiter. Today's set menu (€16): starter, main, dessert. Starters: green salad with walnuts, or leek soup. Mains: pork sausage with lentils (a Lyon speciality), or roast chicken with potatoes, or fish with rice. Desserts: chocolate mousse, or apple tart with almonds, or fromage blanc. When the learner mentions the allergy, check with the chef and say the salad can be made without walnuts, the tart has almonds, and the chocolate mousse is fine. Keep every reply to one or two very short sentences in simple A1 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour ! Vous avez choisi ? Aujourd'hui, il y a le menu du jour.

> Hello! Have you decided? Today there's the set menu.

## Goals

- allergy: Explain your allergy and what you don't eat
- check: Ask what's in a dish
- order: Order a starter, main and dessert you can eat

## Phrases

- Je suis allergique aux noix. | I'm allergic to nuts.
- Je ne mange pas de porc. | I don't eat pork.
- Il y a des noix dans la salade ? | Are there nuts in the salad?
- Vous pouvez faire la salade sans noix ? | Can you make the salad without nuts?
- Je prends le poulet, s'il vous plaît. | I'll have the chicken, please.
- Et en dessert, la mousse au chocolat. | And for dessert, the chocolate mousse.
