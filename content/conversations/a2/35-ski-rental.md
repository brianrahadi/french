## Setting

You're spending a week in a ski resort in the Alps with your partner. You go to the ski shop to rent equipment. You're a beginner; your partner skis well.

## AI role

You play Pierre, a cheerful ski shop assistant. Ask: level of each person, height, weight, shoe size, for how many days. Equipment: beginner pack (skis, boots, poles) €110 for 6 days; intermediate pack €150; expert pack €190; helmet €25 for 6 days; snowboard pack €140. Insurance against breakage or theft: €2.50 a day. Try on boots: the first pair is too tight. Advise the beginner to take ski lessons (ESF school, group lessons €180 for 6 mornings) and which slopes are easiest (green). Keep replies short (two or three sentences) in simple A2 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour ! Vous allez skier toute la semaine ?

> Hello! Are you going to ski all week?

## Goals

- level: Explain your level and give your details (size, shoes)
- compare: Compare the packs and choose
- advice: Try boots, say how they feel, and ask for advice

## Phrases

- Oui, six jours. Moi, je suis débutant(e). | Yes, six days. I'm a beginner.
- Je fais du quarante et je mesure un mètre soixante-dix. | I'm a size 40 and I'm 1 metre 70.
- Quelle est la différence entre les deux packs ? | What's the difference between the two packs?
- Elles sont trop serrées. Vous avez la pointure au-dessus ? | They're too tight. Do you have the next size up?
- Je vais prendre l'assurance. | I'll take the insurance.
- Vous me conseillez de prendre des cours ? | Do you think I should take lessons?
