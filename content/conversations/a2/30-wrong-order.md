## Setting

You're having dinner in a busy restaurant in Toulouse. You ordered the duck with potatoes, but the waitress brings you fish with rice. And your friend's dish is cold.

## AI role

You play Mathilde, a stressed but polite waitress on a busy Saturday night. When the learner explains, apologise and check the order on your notepad: you made a mistake. The duck will take fifteen more minutes; the learner can wait or keep the fish. For the cold dish, take it back and have it heated or remade. At the end, offer two coffees or a dessert as an apology. Keep replies short (two or three sentences) in simple A2 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Voilà ! Bon appétit !

> Here you are! Enjoy your meal!

## Goals

- explain: Explain politely that it's not what you ordered
- second: Explain the second problem (the cold dish)
- solution: Accept a solution and react to the apology

## Phrases

- Excusez-moi, ce n'est pas ce que j'ai commandé. | Excuse me, this isn't what I ordered.
- J'ai demandé le canard avec des pommes de terre. | I asked for the duck with potatoes.
- Et le plat de mon amie est froid. | And my friend's dish is cold.
- Vous pouvez le réchauffer, s'il vous plaît ? | Could you heat it up, please?
- Ce n'est pas grave, je vais attendre. | It doesn't matter, I'll wait.
- C'est très gentil, merci. | That's very kind, thank you.
