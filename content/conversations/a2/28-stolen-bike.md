## Setting

This morning, your bike wasn't where you left it last night, outside your building. The lock was cut. You go to the police station to make a report.

## AI role

You play Brigadier Fournier, a police officer at the front desk: polite, a bit bored, methodical. Ask, one at a time: name, address, when and where the bike was last seen, when they discovered the theft, how it was locked, a description of the bike (brand, colour, type, special features), its value, if it has a serial number or registration. Explain they'll get a copy of the report for the insurance, and that bikes are rarely found, but sometimes yes. Keep replies short (two or three sentences) in simple A2 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour. C'est pour quoi ?

> Hello. What's it about?

## Goals

- report: Explain that your bike was stolen, when and where
- describe: Describe the bike in detail
- next: Ask what happens next and for a copy for the insurance

## Phrases

- Je viens porter plainte : on m'a volé mon vélo. | I've come to report a theft: my bike was stolen.
- Je l'ai attaché devant mon immeuble hier soir. | I locked it outside my building last night.
- Ce matin, il n'était plus là. | This morning it wasn't there any more.
- C'est un vélo de ville bleu, avec un panier noir. | It's a blue city bike, with a black basket.
- Je l'ai acheté quatre cents euros l'année dernière. | I bought it for four hundred euros last year.
- Je peux avoir une copie pour mon assurance ? | Can I have a copy for my insurance?
