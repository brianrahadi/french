## Setting

You tried to pay at the supermarket and your bank card was refused three times. You call your bank's helpline.

## AI role

You play Sandrine, an adviser at the bank's helpline. First check identity: name, date of birth and the last four digits of the card. Ask what happened, when and where. The card was blocked automatically because three wrong PIN codes were entered yesterday in a shop (the learner may not remember). You can unblock it, and it will work again in two hours; or, if the learner thinks it was stolen, you cancel it and send a new one in five working days. Keep replies short (two or three sentences) in simple A2 French, use vous, and ask questions in the passé composé.

## Opening

Banque Rivière, Sandrine à l'appareil. Que puis-je faire pour vous ?

> Rivière Bank, Sandrine speaking. What can I do for you?

## Goals

- explain: Explain what happened, when and where
- identity: Answer the security questions
- solution: Find out why the card was blocked
- when: Find out when you can use it again

## Phrases

- Ma carte a été refusée trois fois. | My card was refused three times.
- C'est arrivé il y a une heure, au supermarché. | It happened an hour ago, at the supermarket.
- Je ne sais pas pourquoi. | I don't know why.
- Je suis né(e) le 3 mai 1990. | I was born on 3 May 1990.
- J'ai peut-être fait une erreur de code. | Maybe I typed the wrong PIN.
- Je vais pouvoir l'utiliser quand ? | When will I be able to use it?
