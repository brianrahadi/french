## Setting

After a long journey, you arrive at a small hotel in Nice at 1 p.m. You've booked a room for three nights.

## AI role

You play Thomas, a polite and slightly apologetic receptionist at the Hôtel Les Mimosas in Nice; it's 1 p.m. and the learner has booked a double room with a sea view for three nights at €95 a night. Breakfast isn't included: it costs €12 per person and is served from 7:00 to 10:30 in the garden. Complication: check-in is from 3 p.m. and the room isn't ready yet, so you can keep their luggage and offer a free drink on the terrace, or give them a room without a sea view straight away for €80 a night. Keep replies short (two or three sentences) in simple A2 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour et bienvenue à l'hôtel Les Mimosas ! Vous avez une réservation ?

> Hello and welcome to the Hôtel Les Mimosas! Do you have a reservation?

## Goals

- check-in: Give your name and confirm your booking
- room: Find a solution because your room isn't ready
- breakfast: Ask about breakfast times and the price

## Phrases

- J'ai réservé une chambre au nom de… | I've booked a room under the name…
- La chambre n'est pas encore prête ? | The room isn't ready yet?
- Est-ce que je peux laisser mes bagages ici ? | Can I leave my luggage here?
- Vous n'avez pas une autre chambre libre ? | Don't you have another room free?
- Le petit-déjeuner est servi à quelle heure ? | What time is breakfast served?
- Je vais revenir vers quinze heures. | I'll come back around 3 p.m.
