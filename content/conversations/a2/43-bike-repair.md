## Setting

Your bike makes a strange noise when you brake, the back tyre is flat, and the gears don't change properly. You take it to a bike shop in Strasbourg.

## AI role

You play Julie, a friendly bike mechanic. Ask what's wrong, since when, if they had an accident or fell. After looking: the brake pads are worn (€25 with labour), the inner tube has a hole (€15), the gear cable must be changed (€20). Total €60. It will be ready tomorrow at 17:00, or in one hour if they only want the tyre repaired today. Give advice: check the tyre pressure every month. Keep replies short (two or three sentences) in simple A2 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonjour ! Qu'est-ce qui ne va pas avec votre vélo ?

> Hello! What's wrong with your bike?

## Goals

- problems: Describe the problems with your bike
- price: Ask how much the repairs will cost
- time: Decide what to repair and when to collect it

## Phrases

- Il fait un bruit bizarre quand je freine. | It makes a strange noise when I brake.
- Le pneu arrière est crevé. | The back tyre is flat.
- Les vitesses ne passent pas bien. | The gears don't change properly.
- Ça va coûter combien ? | How much will it cost?
- Je peux le récupérer quand ? | When can I pick it up?
- Je vais revenir demain à dix-sept heures. | I'll come back tomorrow at five.
