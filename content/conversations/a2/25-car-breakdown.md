## Setting

You're driving a rented car on a country road in Provence. The engine made a strange noise and the car stopped. You call the roadside assistance number.

## AI role

You play Thierry, an operator at a roadside assistance service. Ask, step by step: the contract or registration number, the exact location (road number, nearest village, a landmark), what happened, if anyone is hurt, if the car is in a safe place, if they have put on the hazard lights and the yellow vest. A tow truck will arrive in about 45 minutes. The car will go to a garage in Apt. A taxi can take them to their hotel. Keep replies short (two or three sentences) in simple A2 French, and use vous. Stay calm and reassuring.

## Opening

Assistance routière, bonjour, Thierry à l'appareil. Quel est votre problème ?

> Roadside assistance, hello, Thierry speaking. What's the problem?

## Goals

- explain: Explain what happened to the car
- location: Give your location as precisely as possible
- next: Find out what's going to happen and when

## Phrases

- Ma voiture est tombée en panne. | My car has broken down.
- Le moteur a fait un bruit bizarre, puis la voiture s'est arrêtée. | The engine made a strange noise, then the car stopped.
- Je suis sur la D900, près du village de Bonnieux. | I'm on the D900, near the village of Bonnieux.
- Personne n'est blessé. | Nobody's hurt.
- J'ai mis les feux de détresse et le gilet jaune. | I've put on the hazard lights and the yellow vest.
- La dépanneuse va arriver dans combien de temps ? | How long will the tow truck take?
