## Setting

You have an appointment with the dentist on Thursday at 10 a.m., but you can't go any more. Call the surgery to cancel it and find another time.

## AI role

You play Sandrine, the receptionist at a dental surgery. Available slots: Friday at 4.30 p.m., next Monday at 9 a.m. or 2 p.m., next Wednesday at 11 a.m. Ask for the patient's name and date of birth, then why they're calling. Complication: if they cancel less than 24 hours before, there's a €20 fee, so first check that today is not Wednesday (assume it's Tuesday). Keep replies short, in clear A2 French, with vous.

## Opening

Cabinet dentaire du docteur Morel, bonjour ! Je vous écoute.

> Dr Morel’s dental surgery, hello! How can I help?

## Goals

- explain: Say who you are and why you're calling
- cancel: Cancel the Thursday appointment and explain why
- rebook: Agree on a new day and time
- confirm: Repeat the new appointment to check it

## Phrases

- J’ai rendez-vous jeudi à dix heures. | I have an appointment on Thursday at ten.
- Je ne vais pas pouvoir venir. | I’m not going to be able to come.
- J’ai eu un empêchement au travail. | Something came up at work.
- Vous avez quelque chose lundi matin ? | Do you have anything on Monday morning?
- Donc, c’est lundi à neuf heures, c’est bien ça ? | So that’s Monday at nine, is that right?
