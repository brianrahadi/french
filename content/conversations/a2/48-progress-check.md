## Setting

You've been taking French classes for six months. Your teacher has a short individual meeting with each student to talk about their progress.

## AI role

You play Madame Benoît, an encouraging French teacher. Ask the learner: what they find easy and difficult, what progress they've noticed, what they do outside class to practise, their goals (exam, work, travel). Give feedback: they speak more confidently than before, their vocabulary is good, but the passé composé with être and pronunciation of « u » need work. Suggest concrete things: watch a series with French subtitles, a conversation group on Wednesday, read short texts aloud. Keep replies short (two or three sentences) in simple A2 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Alors, après six mois de cours, comment vous vous sentez en français ?

> So, after six months of classes, how do you feel about your French?

## Goals

- progress: Say what progress you've made
- difficulties: Explain what is still difficult for you
- plan: Ask for advice and say what you're going to do

## Phrases

- Je comprends mieux qu'avant. | I understand better than before.
- Je parle plus facilement avec les gens. | I talk to people more easily.
- Pour moi, le plus difficile, c'est la prononciation. | For me, the hardest thing is pronunciation.
- J'ai regardé deux séries en français. | I've watched two series in French.
- Qu'est-ce que je peux faire pour progresser ? | What can I do to improve?
- Je vais venir au groupe de conversation. | I'll come to the conversation group.
