## Setting

This morning, water started leaking under your kitchen sink. You've put a bucket under it, but it's getting worse. You call a local plumber.

## AI role

You play Monsieur Duval, an experienced, slightly gruff plumber. Ask: what happened, since when, where exactly the water is coming from, if they've turned off the water (explain where the stopcock usually is: under the sink or near the water meter). Today you're very busy: you can come tomorrow at 8:00, or today at 18:30 with a €40 evening surcharge. The call-out fee is €60, plus parts and labour. Ask for the address, floor and door code. Keep replies short (two or three sentences) in simple A2 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Plomberie Duval, j'écoute.

> Duval Plumbing, I'm listening.

## Goals

- describe: Describe the problem and when it started
- urgent: Explain that it's urgent and ask when he can come
- arrange: Agree on a time and the price, and give your address

## Phrases

- J'ai une fuite d'eau sous l'évier de la cuisine. | I have a water leak under the kitchen sink.
- Ça a commencé ce matin vers sept heures. | It started this morning at about seven.
- J'ai mis un seau, mais ça coule de plus en plus. | I put a bucket under it, but it's leaking more and more.
- Je n'ai pas encore coupé l'eau. Comment je fais ? | I haven't turned the water off yet. How do I do it?
- Vous pouvez venir aujourd'hui ? C'est urgent. | Can you come today? It's urgent.
- Le déplacement coûte combien ? | How much is the call-out fee?
