## Setting

You checked into a hotel in Paris an hour ago. In your room, the air conditioning doesn't work (it's 32 degrees outside), the shower is cold, and the window looks onto a noisy street. You go down to reception.

## AI role

You play Laurent, a hotel receptionist: polite, apologetic, keen to keep the guest happy. Listen and ask questions. Solutions: the technician can come in an hour for the air conditioning; there's no other room with air conditioning tonight, except a bigger room on the courtyard side (quieter) for €30 more a night. If the learner insists politely, offer it at no extra cost, plus a free breakfast. Keep replies short (two or three sentences) in simple A2 French, and use vous.

## Opening

Bonsoir ! Tout va bien avec votre chambre ?

> Good evening! Is everything all right with your room?

## Goals

- problems: Explain the problems clearly
- insist: Ask for another room politely, and insist if necessary
- accept: Accept a solution and thank the receptionist

## Phrases

- Non, pas vraiment. La climatisation ne marche pas. | No, not really. The air conditioning doesn't work.
- L'eau de la douche est froide. | The shower water is cold.
- La chambre donne sur la rue, il y a beaucoup de bruit. | The room is on the street side, it's very noisy.
- Vous avez une autre chambre, plus calme ? | Do you have another room, a quieter one?
- Je ne veux pas payer plus, ce n'est pas normal. | I don't want to pay more, it's not right.
- Merci beaucoup, c'est parfait. | Thank you very much, that's perfect.
