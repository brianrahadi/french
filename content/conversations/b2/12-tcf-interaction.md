## Setting

TCF speaking, task 2: the examiner plays a role in an everyday situation and it's your job to lead the conversation by asking questions to get the information you need. Today: you want to sign up for a French course at a language school for the autumn. Ask about the levels, schedule, price, teachers, exams and anything else useful. You have about five minutes.

## AI role

You play Bertrand, the receptionist at a language school, in a TCF task 2 role-play. Information you can give only when asked: group courses at every level from A1 to C1; evening classes (Monday and Wednesday 18:30–20:30) or intensive daytime classes (Monday to Friday 9:00–12:00); 12 students maximum per class; €420 for a 10-week evening term, €890 for 4 weeks intensive; a placement test online before enrolling; TCF and DELF preparation workshops on Saturdays (€25 each); registration deadline next Friday; a 10% discount for students. Answer briefly and only what's asked, so the candidate must keep asking; if they stop, wait or ask "Vous avez d'autres questions ?". Use vous, natural French. Never correct the learner. When they have asked enough or say they've finished, thank them and close the task.

## Opening

Bonjour, école de langues Horizon, je peux vous aider ?

> Hello, Horizon language school, can I help you?

## Goals

- situation: Explain your situation and what you're looking for
- details: Ask about levels, schedules and class size
- price: Ask about prices, discounts and how to pay
- exam: Ask about exam preparation or the placement test
- close: Sum up what you've learned and end politely

## Phrases

- Je voudrais m’inscrire à un cours de français. | I’d like to sign up for a French course.
- Quels sont les horaires des cours du soir ? | What are the times of the evening classes?
- Combien d’étudiants y a-t-il par classe ? | How many students are there per class?
- Est-ce qu’il existe une réduction pour les étudiants ? | Is there a discount for students?
- Comment est-ce que je peux connaître mon niveau ? | How can I find out my level?
- Si j’ai bien compris, … | If I’ve understood correctly, …
