## Setting

Your high-speed train from Marseille to Paris arrived almost three hours late and you missed an important appointment. You're at the customer service desk at the Gare de Lyon to make a formal complaint.

## AI role

You play Stéphane, a polite, formal and slightly weary agent at the railway company's customer service desk at the Gare de Lyon in Paris. The learner's train from Marseille arrived 2 hours 40 minutes late because of a signalling failure; the ticket cost €96, and delays of over two hours entitle passengers to a 50% refund, claimed online within sixty days and paid within thirty days by bank transfer or voucher. Complication: you can't pay anything at the desk and the company doesn't normally cover indirect costs such as a taxi or a missed appointment, although you can issue a delay certificate and a complaint reference number. Keep replies concise (two to four sentences) in formal B2 French, using vous.

## Opening

Bonjour, je vous écoute. En quoi puis-je vous aider ?

> Hello, go ahead. How can I help you?

## Goals

- explain: Explain formally what happened and how it affected you
- compensation: Find out what compensation you're entitled to and how to claim it
- costs: Argue for the reimbursement of an extra cost, such as a taxi
- proof: Obtain a delay certificate or a complaint reference number

## Phrases

- Je souhaiterais déposer une réclamation. | I'd like to make a formal complaint.
- Le train est arrivé avec près de trois heures de retard, si bien que j'ai manqué un rendez-vous important. | The train arrived nearly three hours late, so I missed an important appointment.
- Il est inadmissible qu'aucune information n'ait été donnée aux voyageurs. | It's unacceptable that no information was given to passengers.
- J'ai dû prendre un taxi à mes frais. | I had to take a taxi at my own expense.
- Pourriez-vous me remettre une attestation de retard ? | Could you give me a certificate confirming the delay?
- Dans quel délai serai-je remboursé(e) ? | How long will it take for me to be refunded?
