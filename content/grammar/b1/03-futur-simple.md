## When to use the futur simple

French has two main future tenses. The **futur proche** (*je vais partir*) is the everyday spoken choice for plans and things about to happen. The **futur simple** (*je partirai*) is used for:

- **Predictions** and things that will happen in a more distant or less certain future: *Il fera beau demain. Un jour, tu comprendras.*
- **Promises** and formal commitments: *Je t’appellerai. Je ne le ferai plus.*
- **Writing**, news, instructions and formal speech: *Le magasin sera fermé lundi.*
- After **quand, lorsque, dès que, aussitôt que** when talking about the future (see below).
- In the result part of **si** sentences: *Si tu viens, on ira au cinéma.*

## Formation: infinitive + endings

The stem is the **infinitive** (for -re verbs, drop the final **e**). The endings come from **avoir**: -ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont.

|                | Ending | parler     | finir     | prendre (prendr-) |
| -------------- | ------ | ---------- | --------- | ----------------- |
| je             | -ai    | parlerai   | finirai   | prendrai          |
| tu             | -as    | parleras   | finiras   | prendras          |
| il / elle / on | -a     | parlera    | finira    | prendra           |
| nous           | -ons   | parlerons  | finirons  | prendrons         |
| vous           | -ez    | parlerez   | finirez   | prendrez          |
| ils / elles    | -ont   | parleront  | finiront  | prendront         |

> [!TIP]
> Every future stem ends in **r**, so there’s always an “r” sound before the ending: *parle**r**ai, fini**r**a, prend**r**ons, se**r**ont*. That’s how you recognise the tense.

Spelling-change verbs keep their change in the whole future: *acheter → j’**achèt**erai*, *appeler → j’**appell**erai*, *nettoyer → je **nettoier**ai*, *payer → je **paier**ai* (or *payerai*). But *préférer* keeps its é: *je préférerai*.

## Irregular stems

The endings never change, but about twenty common verbs have an irregular stem. The conditional uses the same stems:

| Verb            | Stem    | Verb             | Stem     |
| --------------- | ------- | ---------------- | -------- |
| être            | ser-    | aller            | ir-      |
| avoir           | aur-    | venir (devenir…) | viendr-  |
| faire           | fer-    | tenir            | tiendr-  |
| pouvoir         | pourr-  | voir             | verr-    |
| vouloir         | voudr-  | envoyer          | enverr-  |
| devoir          | devr-   | courir           | courr-   |
| savoir          | saur-   | mourir           | mourr-   |
| recevoir        | recevr- | falloir          | il faudra |
| s’asseoir       | assiér- | pleuvoir         | il pleuvra |

- Je serai là à 8 heures. | I’ll be there at 8.
- Tu auras ton résultat demain. | You’ll get your result tomorrow.
- Nous irons en Grèce cet été. | We’ll go to Greece this summer.
- Il faudra partir tôt. | We’ll have to leave early.
- On verra ! | We’ll see!

## Quand + future

When the main clause is in the future, French also uses the **future after quand, lorsque, dès que, aussitôt que, tant que**, where English uses the present:

- Quand j’aurai le temps, je t’appellerai. | When I have time, I’ll call you.
- Dès que tu arriveras, on mangera. | As soon as you arrive, we’ll eat.
- Tant qu’il pleuvra, on restera ici. | As long as it rains, we’ll stay here.
- Appelle-moi quand tu seras à Paris. | Call me when you’re in Paris.

> [!WARNING]
> ~~Quand je suis à Paris, je t’appellerai~~ is a common mistake for English speakers. If the time is in the future, both verbs are in the future: ***Quand je serai** à Paris…*

## Si + present → future

In a real (likely) condition, **si** is followed by the **present**, never the future; the result is in the future (or imperative / present):

- Si tu viens, nous irons au cinéma. | If you come, we’ll go to the cinema.
- S’il pleut, on restera à la maison. | If it rains, we’ll stay home.
- Si tu as faim, mange quelque chose. | If you’re hungry, eat something.

**si** becomes **s’** only before **il / ils**: *s’il, s’ils* (but *si elle, si on*).

## The futur antérieur (will have done)

For an action that will be **completed before** another future moment: **avoir / être in the future + past participle**.

- Quand tu arriveras, j’aurai fini. | When you arrive, I’ll have finished.
- Dès que j’aurai reçu ta lettre, je te répondrai. | As soon as I’ve received your letter, I’ll reply.
- À 10 heures, ils seront partis. | By 10, they’ll have left.

## Quick summary

- Infinitive (minus final e) + -ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont.
- Irregular stems: ser-, aur-, fer-, ir-, pourr-, voudr-, devr-, saur-, viendr-, verr-…
- quand / dès que + future when the time is future.
- si + present → future in the result.
