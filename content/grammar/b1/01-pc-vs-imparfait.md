## The core idea

| Passé composé — the events                   | Imparfait — the background                        |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Completed actions, with a clear start or end | Descriptions, feelings, weather, age, time        |
| What happened next (a sequence)              | What was going on (an ongoing action)             |
| A specific number of times: trois fois       | Habits: tous les jours, souvent, d’habitude       |
| Sudden changes: tout à coup, soudain         | States of mind: je pensais, je savais, je voulais |

> [!TIP]
> Think of a film: the **imparfait** is the scenery and the camera rolling; the **passé composé** is what the characters do.

## Together in one sentence

An ongoing action (imparfait) is interrupted by an event (passé composé):

- Je dormais quand le téléphone a sonné. | I was sleeping when the phone rang.
- Il pleuvait, alors nous sommes restés à la maison. | It was raining, so we stayed home.
- Quand j’avais dix ans, je suis allé en Espagne. | When I was ten, I went to Spain.

## Meaning changes

- Je savais. / J’ai su. | I knew. / I found out.
- Je connaissais Paul. / J’ai connu Paul en 2010. | I knew Paul. / I met Paul in 2010.
- Il devait partir. / Il a dû partir. | He was supposed to leave. / He had to leave (and did).

## Exercises

### cloze
- sentence: Je ___ quand le téléphone a sonné.
- hint: dormir
- answer: dormais
- en: I was sleeping when the phone rang.
- explain: Ongoing background action → imparfait.

### cloze
- sentence: Il pleuvait, alors nous ___ à la maison.
- hint: rester
- answer: sommes restés
- answer: sommes restées
- en: It was raining, so we stayed home.
- explain: A completed event → passé composé.

### cloze
- sentence: Quand j’___ petit, j’habitais à Nice.
- hint: être
- answer: étais
- en: When I was little, I lived in Nice.
- explain: Age/description → imparfait.

### cloze
- sentence: Hier, j’___ trois fois.
- hint: appeler, you called
- answer: ai appelé
- en: Yesterday I called three times.
- explain: A specific number of times → passé composé.

### cloze
- sentence: D’habitude, nous ___ le bus.
- hint: prendre
- answer: prenions
- en: We usually took the bus.
- explain: Habit → imparfait.

### cloze
- sentence: Tout à coup, la lumière ___.
- hint: s’éteindre
- answer: s’est éteinte
- en: Suddenly, the light went out.
- explain: Sudden event → passé composé (reflexive → être, agreement).

### mcq
- prompt: Choose the natural sentence.
- [ ] Il a fait beau et les oiseaux chantaient.
- [x] Il faisait beau et les oiseaux chantaient.
- [ ] Il faisait beau et les oiseaux ont chanté.
- explain: Pure scene-setting → both verbs in the imparfait.

### mcq
- prompt: “J’ai su la vérité hier” means…
- [ ] I knew the truth yesterday.
- [x] I found out the truth yesterday.
- explain: savoir in the passé composé = to find out.

### mcq
- prompt: When I arrived, everyone was dancing.
- [x] Quand je suis arrivé, tout le monde dansait.
- [ ] Quand j’arrivais, tout le monde a dansé.
- [ ] Quand je suis arrivé, tout le monde a dansé.
- explain: Event (arrival) interrupts an ongoing action (dancing).

### cloze
- sentence: Elle ___ un livre quand je suis entré.
- hint: lire
- answer: lisait
- en: She was reading a book when I came in.
- explain: Ongoing action interrupted → imparfait.
