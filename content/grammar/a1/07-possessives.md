## The forms

|                 | Masc. singular | Fem. singular | Plural |
| --------------- | -------------- | ------------- | ------ |
| my              | mon            | ma            | mes    |
| your (tu)       | ton            | ta            | tes    |
| his / her / its | son            | sa            | ses    |
| our             | notre          | notre         | nos    |
| your (vous)     | votre          | votre         | vos    |
| their           | leur           | leur          | leurs  |

## Agreement with the object

The possessive agrees with the **noun that follows**, not with the owner. So **sa mère** can mean *his mother* or *her mother*; **son père** can mean *his father* or *her father*.

- Julie aime son frère. | Julie loves her brother.
- Marc adore sa voiture. | Marc loves his car.

> [!TIP]
> To make it clear, add **à lui / à elle**: *C’est sa voiture à elle.*

## ma, ta, sa before a vowel

Before a feminine noun starting with a vowel or silent h, use **mon, ton, son** to avoid two vowels clashing: *mon amie, ton école, son histoire*.

## Exercises

### cloze
- sentence: C’est ___ frère.
- hint: my
- answer: mon
- en: This is my brother.
- explain: frère is masculine: mon.

### cloze
- sentence: ___ sœur s’appelle Léa.
- hint: my
- answer: Ma
- en: My sister is called Léa.
- explain: sœur is feminine: ma.

### cloze
- sentence: Je te présente ___ amie Julie.
- hint: my
- answer: mon
- en: Let me introduce my friend Julie.
- explain: Feminine noun starting with a vowel → mon.

### cloze
- sentence: Paul adore ___ voiture.
- hint: his
- answer: sa
- en: Paul loves his car.
- explain: voiture is feminine → sa, even though Paul is a man.

### cloze
- sentence: Marie parle avec ___ père.
- hint: her
- answer: son
- en: Marie is talking to her father.
- explain: père is masculine → son.

### cloze
- sentence: Les enfants jouent avec ___ chien.
- hint: their
- answer: leur
- en: The children are playing with their dog.
- explain: One dog → leur.

### cloze
- sentence: Ils adorent ___ enfants.
- hint: their
- answer: leurs
- en: They adore their children.
- explain: Several children → leurs.

### cloze
- sentence: Vous avez ___ billets ?
- hint: your, formal
- answer: vos
- en: Do you have your tickets?
- explain: vous + plural noun → vos.

### mcq
- prompt: “sa maison” can mean…
- [ ] his house
- [ ] her house
- [x] his or her house
- explain: The possessive agrees with maison (f), not the owner.

### translate
- en: Our parents are here.
- answer: Nos parents sont ici
- answer: Nos parents sont là
