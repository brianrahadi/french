## Stem + ending

Remove **-er** from the infinitive to get the stem (*parler → parl-*), then add the endings:

|                | Ending | parler  | aimer  |
| -------------- | ------ | ------- | ------ |
| je (j’)        | -e     | parle   | aime   |
| tu             | -es    | parles  | aimes  |
| il / elle / on | -e     | parle   | aime   |
| nous           | -ons   | parlons | aimons |
| vous           | -ez    | parlez  | aimez  |
| ils / elles    | -ent   | parlent | aiment |

> [!TIP]
> **parle, parles, parle, parlent** all sound the same — the -es and -ent endings are silent. Only the nous and vous forms sound different.

## One tense, three English meanings

French has no separate continuous tense. *Je parle* means **I speak**, **I’m speaking** and **I do speak**. To stress “right now”, you can say *je suis en train de parler*.

- Nous habitons à Lyon. | We live in Lyon.
- Qu’est-ce que tu regardes ? | What are you watching?
- Ils travaillent le samedi. | They work on Saturdays.

## Spelling changes to keep the sound

| Verb type            | Change                | Example                     |
| -------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------------- |
| -ger                 | nous + e              | manger → nous mangeons      |
| -cer                 | nous: c → ç           | commencer → nous commençons |
| e + consonant + er   | e → è (not nous/vous) | acheter → j’achète          |
| é + consonant + er   | é → è (not nous/vous) | préférer → je préfère       |
| -eler / -eter (most) | double consonant      | appeler → j’appelle         |
| -oyer / -uyer        | y → i                 | nettoyer → je nettoie       |

These changes happen only when the ending is silent (je, tu, il, ils). The nous and vous forms keep the infinitive’s spelling: *nous achetons, vous préférez*.

## Exercises

### cloze
- sentence: Nous ___ français.
- hint: parler
- answer: parlons
- en: We speak French.
- explain: nous → -ons.

### cloze
- sentence: Ils ___ à Paris.
- hint: habiter
- answer: habitent
- en: They live in Paris.
- explain: ils → -ent (silent).

### cloze
- sentence: Tu ___ la télé ?
- hint: regarder
- answer: regardes
- en: Are you watching TV?
- explain: tu → -es.

### cloze
- sentence: Vous ___ le jazz ?
- hint: aimer
- answer: aimez
- en: Do you like jazz?
- explain: vous → -ez.

### cloze
- sentence: Nous ___ à midi.
- hint: manger
- answer: mangeons
- en: We eat at noon.
- explain: -ger verbs keep an e before -ons to keep the soft g sound: mangeons.

### cloze
- sentence: Nous ___ le cours.
- hint: commencer
- answer: commençons
- en: We are starting the class.
- explain: -cer verbs take ç before o: commençons.

### cloze
- sentence: Elle ___ des pommes.
- hint: acheter
- answer: achète
- en: She is buying apples.
- explain: acheter: e → è when the ending is silent.

### cloze
- sentence: Je m’___ Sophie.
- hint: appeler
- answer: appelle
- en: My name is Sophie.
- explain: appeler doubles the l when the ending is silent: j’appelle.

### mcq
- prompt: “Je travaille” can mean…
- [ ] I work
- [ ] I am working
- [x] Both
- explain: The French present covers both the simple and the continuous present.

- words: Ils écoutent de la musique
