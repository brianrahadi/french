## Subject pronouns

| Singular  |                          | Plural      |                         |
| --------- | ------------------------ | ----------- | ----------------------- |
| je (j’)   | I                        | nous        | we                      |
| tu        | you (informal)           | vous        | you (plural / formal)   |
| il / elle | he, it / she, it         | ils / elles | they (m. or mixed / f.) |
| on        | we (spoken), one, people |             |                         |

- **tu** is for friends, family, children and pets. **vous** is for strangers, colleagues you don’t know well, and anyone older or senior — and for any group.
- **on** is used constantly in speech to mean “we”: *On va au cinéma ?* It always takes the il/elle form of the verb.
- **ils** covers any group with at least one masculine noun or person. **elles** is only for all-feminine groups.

## Être — to be

|                | être   |
| -------------- | ------ |
| je             | suis   |
| tu             | es     |
| il / elle / on | est    |
| nous           | sommes |
| vous           | êtes   |
| ils / elles    | sont   |

- Je suis canadien. | I am Canadian.
- Vous êtes prêts ? | Are you ready?
- Elles sont au bureau. | They are at the office.

## Avoir — to have

|                | avoir |
| -------------- | ----- |
| j’             | ai    |
| tu             | as    |
| il / elle / on | a     |
| nous           | avons |
| vous           | avez  |
| ils / elles    | ont   |

**je** becomes **j’** before a vowel or a silent h: *j’ai, j’habite*. Note the liaison in *nous‿avons, vous‿avez, ils‿ont* (a z sound) — it distinguishes **ils ont** (they have) from **ils sont** (they are).

## Where English says “be” but French says “have”

- J’ai 30 ans. | I am 30 (years old).
- Tu as faim ? Tu as soif ? | Are you hungry? Are you thirsty?
- J’ai froid. / J’ai chaud. | I’m cold. / I’m hot.
- Il a peur des chiens. | He is afraid of dogs.
- Vous avez raison. | You are right.

> [!WARNING]
> Never say ~~Je suis 30 ans~~ or ~~Je suis froid~~ (which means “I am a cold person”). Use **avoir**.

## Exercises

### cloze
- sentence: Nous ___ étudiants.
- hint: être
- answer: sommes
- en: We are students.
- explain: nous → sommes.

### cloze
- sentence: Tu ___ un chien ?
- hint: avoir
- answer: as
- en: Do you have a dog?
- explain: tu → as (the s is silent).

### cloze
- sentence: Ils ___ en retard.
- hint: être
- answer: sont
- en: They are late.
- explain: ils → sont. Don’t confuse with ils ont (they have).

### cloze
- sentence: Elle ___ vingt ans.
- hint: avoir
- answer: a
- en: She is twenty.
- explain: Age uses avoir: elle a vingt ans.

### mcq
- prompt: How do you say “I am 30 years old”?
- [ ] Je suis 30 ans.
- [x] J’ai 30 ans.
- [ ] J’ai 30 années.
- [ ] Je suis 30 années.
- explain: French uses avoir + ans for age.

### mcq
- prompt: You’re meeting your new manager for the first time. Which pronoun do you use?
- [ ] tu
- [x] vous
- explain: vous is the polite form for people you don’t know well or who are senior to you.

### cloze
- sentence: Vous ___ fatigués ?
- hint: être
- answer: êtes
- en: Are you tired?
- explain: vous → êtes (with a circumflex).

### cloze
- sentence: On ___ faim !
- hint: avoir
- answer: a
- en: We’re hungry!
- explain: on takes the il/elle form: on a.

### mcq
- prompt: Which sentence means “I’m cold”?
- [ ] Je suis froid.
- [x] J’ai froid.
- [ ] Je fais froid.
- explain: Physical sensations use avoir: j’ai froid, j’ai chaud, j’ai faim.

### order
- en: We are at home.
- words: Nous sommes à la maison
- extra: avons
- punct: .

### translate
- en: They (all women) are French.
- answer: Elles sont françaises
- explain: elles for an all-female group; françaises agrees (feminine plural).
