## Every noun has a gender

French nouns are either **masculine** or **feminine** — even objects and ideas. The gender changes the article, the adjectives and the pronouns, so always learn a noun **with its article**: not *maison* but *la maison*.

## Definite articles — the

|                           | Singular                | Plural     |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------- |
| Masculine                 | le livre                | les livres |
| Feminine                  | la table                | les tables |
| Before a vowel / silent h | l’ami, l’école, l’hôtel | les‿amis   |

Use the definite article for specific things (*le livre de Paul*) **and** for things in general, especially after verbs of liking: *J’aime **le** chocolat* (I like chocolate), *Le sport est bon pour la santé*.

## Indefinite articles — a, some

|           | Singular  | Plural     |
| --------- | --------- | ---------- |
| Masculine | un livre  | des livres |
| Feminine  | une table | des tables |

> [!TIP]
> **des** is often needed where English uses nothing: *J’ai **des** amis à Paris* — I have friends in Paris.

## Clues to guess the gender

| Usually feminine         | Usually masculine   |
| ------------------------ | ------------------- |
| -tion, -sion (la nation) | -ment (le moment)   |
| -té (la liberté)         | -age (le fromage)   |
| -ure (la voiture)        | -eau (le bateau)    |
| -ette (la baguette)      | -isme (le tourisme) |
| -ence, -ance (la chance) | -oir (le miroir)    |

> [!WARNING]
> There are exceptions: **la** plage, **la** page, **l’eau** (f), **la** peau — and **le** problème, **le** système (Greek words in -ème are masculine).

## Plurals

- Most nouns add a silent **-s**: *un chat → des chats*.
- **-eau / -eu → -x**: *un gâteau → des gâteaux*, *un jeu → des jeux*.
- **-al → -aux**: *un journal → des journaux*, *un animal → des animaux*.
- Nouns ending in -s, -x, -z don’t change: *un pays → des pays*.

## Exercises

### mcq
- prompt: Choose the article.
- sentence: ___ maison
- [ ] le
- [x] la
- [ ] l’
- explain: maison is feminine: la maison.

### mcq
- prompt: Choose the article.
- sentence: ___ problème
- [x] le
- [ ] la
- explain: Words in -ème from Greek are masculine: le problème, le système.

### cloze
- sentence: J’aime ___ chocolat.
- answer: le
- en: I like chocolate.
- explain: Likes and dislikes take the definite article (general sense).

### cloze
- sentence: Il y a ___ pharmacie près d’ici ?
- answer: une
- en: Is there a pharmacy near here?
- explain: a/an + feminine noun → une.

### cloze
- sentence: ___ enfants jouent dans le parc.
- answer: les
- en: The children are playing in the park.
- explain: Plural definite article: les (with liaison: les‿enfants).

### mcq

- explain: Nouns ending in -tion are feminine: la nation.

### mcq

- [ ] le
