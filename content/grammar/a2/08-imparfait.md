## What the imparfait is for

The **imparfait** is the second main past tense. The passé composé tells you **what happened** (events); the imparfait tells you **what things were like** (background, habits, ongoing situations). Think of a film: the imparfait is the scenery and the music, the passé composé is the action.

It corresponds to several English forms: *je parlais* = **I was speaking**, **I used to speak**, **I would speak** (habit), and often simply **I spoke** in descriptions.

## Formation: nous-stem + endings

Take the **nous** form of the present, remove **-ons**, and add the endings. It works for every verb, regular or irregular, except **être** (stem **ét-**).

|                | Ending | parler (parl-) | finir (finiss-) | faire (fais-) | être (ét-) |
| -------------- | ------ | -------------- | --------------- | ------------- | ---------- |
| je             | -ais   | parlais        | finissais       | faisais       | étais      |
| tu             | -ais   | parlais        | finissais       | faisais       | étais      |
| il / elle / on | -ait   | parlait        | finissait       | faisait       | était      |
| nous           | -ions  | parlions       | finissions      | faisions      | étions     |
| vous           | -iez   | parliez        | finissiez       | faisiez       | étiez      |
| ils / elles    | -aient | parlaient      | finissaient     | faisaient     | étaient    |

More stems: *avoir → av-* (j’avais), *aller → all-* (j’allais), *prendre → pren-* (je prenais), *boire → buv-* (je buvais), *voir → voy-* (je voyais), *lire → lis-* (je lisais), *dire → dis-* (je disais). Impersonal verbs: *il pleuvait, il fallait, il y avait*.

> [!TIP]
> Four of the six endings (-ais, -ais, -ait, -aient) sound the same: “-ay”. Spelling: *manger → je mangeais* (but *nous mangions*), *commencer → je commençais* (but *nous commencions*). The e and ç keep the sound before a.

## Use 1: descriptions and background

Weather, time, age, appearance, feelings, physical and mental states, places: anything that sets the scene.

- Il faisait beau et le ciel était bleu. | The weather was nice and the sky was blue.
- Il était minuit. | It was midnight.
- J’avais dix ans. | I was ten.
- Elle portait une robe rouge. | She was wearing a red dress.
- J’étais fatigué et j’avais faim. | I was tired and hungry.
- La maison était grande et il y avait un jardin. | The house was big and there was a garden.

## Use 2: habits and repeated actions

What you **used to do**, regularly, with no fixed end:

- Quand j’étais petit, je jouais au foot tous les jours. | When I was little, I played football every day.
- Le dimanche, nous allions chez ma grand-mère. | On Sundays we would go to my grandmother’s.
- Avant, je fumais. | I used to smoke.

Signal words: **avant** (before, in the past), **autrefois**, **à l’époque**, **d’habitude**, **souvent**, **toujours**, **chaque jour**, **le lundi**, **tous les étés**, **quand j’étais petit**.

## Use 3: actions in progress

An action that **was going on** when something else happened (the interruption is in the passé composé), or two actions going on at the same time:

- Je lisais quand tu as appelé. | I was reading when you called.
- Pendant que je travaillais, il dormait. | While I was working, he was sleeping.
- Qu’est-ce que tu faisais ? | What were you doing?

## Use 4: politeness, suggestions and wishes

- Je voulais vous demander quelque chose. | I wanted to ask you something. (softer than je veux)
- Si on allait au cinéma ? | How about going to the cinema?
- Si seulement j’avais plus de temps ! | If only I had more time!
- Si j’étais riche, je voyagerais. | If I were rich, I would travel.

## After venir de and être en train de

In the past, these two constructions use the imparfait: *Je venais d’arriver quand il a appelé* (I had just arrived when he called). *J’étais en train de cuisiner* (I was busy cooking).

## Quick summary

- Stem = nous form minus -ons (except être: ét-) + -ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient.
- Descriptions, habits, actions in progress, polite requests, si + suggestion.
- Signal words: avant, d’habitude, souvent, tous les jours, quand j’étais petit.

## Goals

- formation: Form the imparfait from the nous stem § Formation: nous-stem + endings
- description: Describe the past: background, weather, feelings § Use 1: descriptions and background
- habits: Talk about past habits (used to) § Use 2: habits and repeated actions
- progress: Describe an action in progress (was doing) § Use 3: actions in progress
- politeness: Soften requests and make suggestions (Si on allait…) § Use 4: politeness, suggestions and wishes

- sentence: Quand j’étais petit, je ___ au foot.
- hint: jouer
- answer: jouais
- en: When I was little, I used to play football.
- explain: Habit in the past → imparfait.
- goal: habits
