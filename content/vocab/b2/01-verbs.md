---
id: b2-verbs
title: Nuanced verbs
titleFr: Verbes nuancés
---

| French               | English                                | Type | Example                                       | Translation                                     | Note                                             |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ---- | --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| s'avérer             | to turn out (to be)                    | v    | L'hypothèse s'est avérée fausse.              | The hypothesis turned out to be false.          |                                                  |
| aborder              | to tackle, to address (a topic)        | v    | Nous aborderons ce point demain.              | We'll address this point tomorrow.              |                                                  |
| souligner            | to underline, to stress                | v    | Je tiens à souligner ce détail.               | I want to stress this detail.                   |                                                  |
| susciter             | to arouse, to spark                    | v    | Sa décision a suscité la polémique.           | His decision sparked controversy.               |                                                  |
| remettre en question | to call into question                  | expr | Cette étude remet en question nos certitudes. | This study calls our certainties into question. |                                                  |
| tenir compte de      | to take into account                   | expr | Il faut tenir compte du budget.               | We must take the budget into account.           |                                                  |
| faire face à         | to face, to cope with                  | expr | Le pays fait face à une crise.                | The country is facing a crisis.                 |                                                  |
| s'engager            | to commit (oneself)                    | v    | Il s'est engagé à nous aider.                 | He committed to helping us.                     |                                                  |
| se méfier de         | to be wary of, to distrust             | v    | Méfie-toi des apparences.                     | Beware of appearances.                          |                                                  |
| prétendre            | to claim                               | v    | Il prétend tout savoir.                       | He claims to know everything.                   | False friend: not "to pretend" (faire semblant). |
| envisager            | to consider, to envisage               | v    | J'envisage de changer de travail.             | I'm considering changing jobs.                  |                                                  |
| entraîner            | to lead to, to cause; to train         | v    | Cette mesure entraînera des hausses de prix.  | This measure will lead to price rises.          |                                                  |
| constater            | to note, to observe                    | v    | On constate une amélioration.                 | We note an improvement.                         |                                                  |
| estimer              | to consider, to estimate               | v    | J'estime que c'est injuste.                   | I consider it unfair.                           |                                                  |
| relever              | to take up (a challenge); to point out | v    | Elle a relevé le défi.                        | She rose to the challenge.                      |                                                  |
| atteindre            | to reach, to achieve                   | v    | Nous avons atteint nos objectifs.             | We've reached our targets.                      |                                                  |
| soutenir             | to support                             | v    | Mes amis m’ont toujours soutenu.              | My friends have always supported me.            |                                                  |
| accroître            | to increase                            | v    | Il faut accroître la production.              | Production must be increased.                   |                                                  |
| réduire              | to reduce                              | v    | Réduire les émissions de CO₂.                 | To reduce CO₂ emissions.                        |                                                  |
| renforcer            | to strengthen                          | v    | Ce voyage a renforcé notre amitié.            | This trip strengthened our friendship.          |                                                  |
| exiger               | to demand, to require                  | v    | Ce poste exige beaucoup de patience.          | This job requires a lot of patience.            |                                                  |
| parvenir à           | to manage to, to reach                 | v    | Je ne parviens pas à dormir.                  | I can't manage to sleep.                        |                                                  |
| se passer de         | to do without                          | v    | Je ne peux pas me passer de café.             | I can't do without coffee.                      |                                                  |
| faire semblant       | to pretend                             | expr | Il fait semblant de dormir.                   | He's pretending to sleep.                       |                                                  |
