title: Useful verbs
titleFr: Verbes utiles
---

| French           | English                        | Type | Example                                   | Translation                              | Note                                       |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------ | ---- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| réussir          | to succeed; to pass (exam)     | v    | Il a réussi son permis.                   | He passed his driving test.              |                                            |
| échouer          | to fail                        | v    | Le projet a échoué.                       | The project failed.                      |                                            |
| améliorer        | to improve                     | v    | Je veux améliorer mon accent.             | I want to improve my accent.             |                                            |
| permettre        | to allow, to enable            | v    | Internet permet de travailler à distance. | The internet makes remote work possible. |                                            |
| empêcher         | to prevent                     | v    | Le bruit m'empêche de dormir.             | The noise stops me from sleeping.        |                                            |
| éviter           | to avoid                       | v    | J'évite le sucre.                         | I avoid sugar.                           |                                            |
| profiter de      | to make the most of, to enjoy  | v    | Profite bien de tes vacances !            | Enjoy your holiday!                      |                                            |
| se rendre compte | to realize                     | v    | Je me suis rendu compte de mon erreur.    | I realized my mistake.                   |                                            |
| s'occuper de     | to take care of, to deal with  | v    | Je m'occupe des billets.                  | I'll take care of the tickets.           |                                            |
| se plaindre      | to complain                    | v    | Il se plaint tout le temps.               | He complains all the time.               |                                            |
| se tromper       | to be wrong, to make a mistake | v    | Désolé, je me suis trompé de numéro.      | Sorry, I dialled the wrong number.       |                                            |
| se débrouiller   | to manage, to get by           | v    | Je me débrouille en français.             | I get by in French.                      |                                            |
| se mettre à      | to start (doing)               | v    | Il s’est mis à pleuvoir.                  | It started raining.                      |                                            |
| réaliser         | to carry out, to achieve       | v    | Elle a réalisé son rêve.                  | She achieved her dream.                  |                                            |
| prévoir          | to plan, to forecast           | v    | Qu’est-ce que tu as prévu ce week-end ?   | What have you planned this weekend?      |                                            |
| remarquer        | to notice                      | v    | Tu as remarqué quelque chose ?            | Did you notice anything?                 |                                            |
| supporter        | to stand, to put up with       | v    | Je ne supporte pas le bruit.              | I can't stand noise.                     | False friend: not "to support" (soutenir). |
| rendre visite à  | to visit (a person)            | expr | Je rends visite à ma grand-mère.          | I'm visiting my grandmother.             |                                            |
| compter sur      | to count on                    | v    | Tu peux compter sur moi.                  | You can count on me.                     |                                            |
| décider          | to decide                      | v    | J'ai décidé de partir.                    | I've decided to leave.                   |                                            |
| proposer         | to suggest, to offer           | v    | Je te propose un café ?                   | Can I offer you a coffee?                |                                            |
| refuser          | to refuse, to turn down        | v    | Il a refusé l’offre.                      | He turned down the offer.                |                                            |
| convaincre       | to convince                    | v    | Tu m’as convaincu.                        | You've convinced me.                     |                                            |
| partager         | to share                       | v    | On partage un dessert ?                   | Shall we share a dessert?                |                                            |
| se passer        | to happen                      | v    | Qu'est-ce qui se passe ?                  | What's happening?                        |                                            |
