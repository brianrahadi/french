---
id: a1-verbs
title: Essential verbs
titleFr: Les verbes essentiels
---

| French     | English                  | Type | Example                             | Translation                           | Note                                         |
| ---------- | ------------------------ | ---- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| être       | to be                    | v    | Je suis étudiant.                   | I'm a student.                        |                                              |
| avoir      | to have                  | v    | J'ai deux chats.                    | I have two cats.                      |                                              |
| aller      | to go                    | v    | Je vais au travail en vélo.         | I go to work by bike.                 |                                              |
| faire      | to do, to make           | v    | Qu'est-ce que tu fais ce week-end ? | What are you doing this weekend?      |                                              |
| dire       | to say, to tell          | v    | Il dit toujours la vérité.          | He always tells the truth.            |                                              |
| pouvoir    | can, to be able to       | v    | Tu peux m’aider ?                   | Can you help me?                      |                                              |
| vouloir    | to want                  | v    | Je veux apprendre le français.      | I want to learn French.               |                                              |
| savoir     | to know (facts, how to)  | v    | Je ne sais pas nager.               | I can't swim.                         |                                              |
| venir      | to come                  | v    | Tu viens avec nous ?                | Are you coming with us?               |                                              |
| prendre    | to take; to have (food)  | v    | Je prends le métro tous les jours.  | I take the metro every day.           |                                              |
| voir       | to see                   | v    | On se voit demain ?                 | See you tomorrow?                     |                                              |
| parler     | to speak, to talk        | v    | Elle parle trois langues.           | She speaks three languages.           |                                              |
| aimer      | to like, to love         | v    | J'aime beaucoup ce film.            | I really like this film.              |                                              |
| manger     | to eat                   | v    | Nous mangeons à midi.               | We eat at noon.                       |                                              |
| boire      | to drink                 | v    | Tu bois du café ?                   | Do you drink coffee?                  |                                              |
| habiter    | to live (reside)         | v    | J'habite à Montréal.                | I live in Montreal.                   |                                              |
| travailler | to work                  | v    | Il travaille dans un hôpital.       | He works in a hospital.               |                                              |
| donner     | to give                  | v    | Donne-moi ta main.                  | Give me your hand.                    |                                              |
| trouver    | to find                  | v    | Je ne trouve pas mes lunettes.      | I can't find my glasses.              |                                              |
| penser     | to think                 | v    | Je pense que tu as raison.          | I think you are right.                |                                              |
| regarder   | to watch, to look at     | v    | On regarde un film ce soir ?        | Shall we watch a film tonight?        |                                              |
| écouter    | to listen (to)           | v    | J'écoute un podcast en français.    | I'm listening to a podcast in French. | No preposition: écouter la radio.            |
| acheter    | to buy                   | v    | J'achète des fleurs pour ma mère.   | I'm buying flowers for my mother.     |                                              |
| comprendre | to understand            | v    | Je ne comprends pas la question.    | I don't understand the question.      |                                              |
| connaître  | to know (people, places) | v    | Tu connais Paris ?                  | Do you know Paris?                    |                                              |
| devoir     | must, to have to         | v    | Je dois partir à 17 heures.         | I have to leave at 5 p.m.             |                                              |
| mettre     | to put, to put on        | v    | Mets ton manteau, il fait froid.    | Put your coat on, it's cold.          |                                              |
| partir     | to leave                 | v    | Le train part à midi.               | The train leaves at noon.             |                                              |
| aimer bien | to like (people, things) | expr | J'aime bien ton collègue.           | I like your colleague.                | Softer than aimer when talking about people. |
