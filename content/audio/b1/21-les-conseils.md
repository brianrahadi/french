## Scene

Your friend Emma can’t decide whether to take a job in another city. She asks for your advice. You’ll learn several ways to give advice, from gentle to direct.

## Dialogue

- Emma: J’ai besoin d’un conseil. On m’a proposé un poste à Marseille. | I need some advice. I’ve been offered a job in Marseille.
- Alex: C’est super ! Tu hésites ? | That’s great! Are you hesitating?
- Emma: Oui, j’ai peur de quitter mes amis. | Yes, I’m afraid of leaving my friends.
- Alex: Si j’étais toi, j’accepterais. C’est une belle occasion. | If I were you, I’d accept. It’s a great opportunity.
- Emma: Tu crois ? | Do you think so?
- Alex: Oui. Tu devrais au moins visiter la ville avant de décider. | Yes. You should at least visit the city before deciding.
- Emma: C’est vrai. Et si je ne m’y plais pas ? | That’s true. And what if I don’t like it there?
- Alex: À ta place, je ne m’inquiéterais pas. Tu pourras toujours revenir ! | In your shoes, I wouldn’t worry. You can always come back!

## Phrases

- J’ai besoin · d’un conseil | I need some advice
- Tu hésites ? | Are you hesitating?
- J’ai peur · de quitter · mes amis | I’m afraid of leaving my friends
- Si j’étais toi · j’accepterais | If I were you, I’d accept | Imagined situations: the past background tense after “if”, then the conditional.
- une belle occasion | a great opportunity
- Tu crois ? | Do you think so?
- Tu devrais · visiter la ville | You should visit the city
- avant de décider | before deciding
- À ta place | In your shoes | Literally, “in your place”.
- Je ne m’inquiéterais pas | I wouldn’t worry

## Practice

- Si j’étais toi, je visiterais la ville. | Say if you were her, you’d visit the city.
- Tu devrais accepter le poste. | Say she should accept the job.
- À ta place, je n’hésiterais pas. | Say that in her shoes, you wouldn’t hesitate.
- J’ai besoin d’un conseil. Tu crois que je devrais partir ? | Say you need advice, and ask if she thinks you should leave.
- Avant de décider, tu devrais parler à tes amis. | Say she should talk to her friends before deciding.
