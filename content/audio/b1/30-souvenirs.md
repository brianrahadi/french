## Scene

You’re looking at old photos with your friend Paul. He asks about your childhood. You’ll describe how things used to be, with the past tense for habits.

## Dialogue

- Paul: C’est toi sur la photo ? Tu avais quel âge ? | Is that you in the photo? How old were you?
- Alex: J’avais huit ans. Quand j’étais enfant, on habitait à la campagne. | I was eight. When I was a child, we lived in the countryside.
- Paul: Ça te plaisait ? | Did you like it?
- Alex: Oui, beaucoup. Tous les étés, on allait chez mes grands-parents, au bord de la mer. | Yes, a lot. Every summer, we went to my grandparents’ house, by the sea.
- Paul: Qu’est-ce que vous faisiez ? | What did you do?
- Alex: On se baignait tous les jours, et le soir, on jouait aux cartes. | We swam every day, and in the evening, we played cards.
- Paul: Ça a l’air génial. | It sounds great.
- Alex: Oui. Je me souviens encore de l’odeur de la cuisine de ma grand-mère. Ça me manque. | Yes. I still remember the smell of my grandmother’s cooking. I miss it.

## Phrases

- Tu avais · quel âge ? | How old were you? | In French, you “have” an age.
- J’avais huit ans | I was eight
- Quand j’étais enfant | When I was a child
- On habitait · à la campagne | We lived in the countryside | For habits and how things used to be, this past tense ends in a sound like “ay”.
- Tous les étés | every summer
- On allait · chez mes grands-parents | We went to my grandparents’ house
- au bord de la mer | by the sea
- On se baignait · tous les jours | We swam every day
- Je me souviens · de l’odeur | I remember the smell
- Ça me manque | I miss it | Literally, “it is missing to me”. The thing you miss comes first.

## Practice

- Quand j’étais enfant, on allait à la mer tous les étés. | Say that when you were a child, you went to the sea every summer.
- J’avais dix ans, et on habitait à Paris. | Say you were ten, and you lived in Paris.
- Le soir, on jouait aux cartes avec mes grands-parents. | Say that in the evening, you played cards with your grandparents.
- Je me souviens de la maison. Ça me manque. | Say you remember the house, and you miss it.
- Tu avais quel âge, et vous habitiez où ? | Ask how old your friend was, and where they lived.
