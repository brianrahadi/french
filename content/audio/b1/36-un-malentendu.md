## Scene

Your colleague Sarah is annoyed: the report she needed this morning isn’t ready. There has been a misunderstanding with your manager, Pierre. You explain what he told you. You’ll practise reporting what someone said.

## Dialogue

- Sarah: Alex, le rapport n’est pas prêt ? J’en avais besoin pour la réunion de ce matin ! | Alex, the report isn’t ready? I needed it for this morning’s meeting!
- Alex: Je suis désolé, il y a eu un malentendu. Pierre m’a dit que la réunion était jeudi. | I’m sorry, there’s been a misunderstanding. Pierre told me the meeting was on Thursday.
- Sarah: Jeudi ? Mais je lui avais écrit que c’était mardi. | Thursday? But I’d written to him that it was Tuesday.
- Alex: Il m’a aussi demandé si je pouvais ajouter les chiffres de mars. Alors j’ai attendu. | He also asked me if I could add the March figures. So I waited.
- Sarah: Bon. Tu crois que tu peux le finir avant midi ? | Right. Do you think you can finish it before noon?
- Alex: Oui, je m’y mets tout de suite. Je te l’envoie dès qu’il sera prêt. | Yes, I’ll get on with it right away. I’ll send it to you as soon as it’s ready.
- Sarah: Merci. Et la prochaine fois, demande-moi directement ! | Thanks. And next time, ask me directly!
- Alex: Promis. On évitera ce genre de problème. | Promise. We’ll avoid this kind of problem.

## Phrases

- Il y a eu · un malentendu | There’s been a misunderstanding
- Pierre m’a dit · que la réunion · était jeudi | Pierre told me the meeting was on Thursday | After a past verb of saying, “is” becomes “was”, just like in English.
- Je lui avais écrit · que c’était mardi | I had written to him that it was Tuesday
- Il m’a demandé · si je pouvais · ajouter les chiffres | He asked me if I could add the figures
- J’en avais besoin | I needed it
- Tu crois que · tu peux le finir · avant midi ? | Do you think you can finish it before noon?
- Je m’y mets · tout de suite | I’ll get on with it right away
- Je te l’envoie · dès qu’il sera prêt | I’ll send it to you as soon as it’s ready | After the word for “as soon as”, French uses the future when the action is still to come.
- Demande-moi · directement | Ask me directly
- Promis | Promise

## Practice

- Elle m’a dit qu’elle était malade. | Say she told you she was ill.
- Il m’a demandé si j’étais libre lundi. | Say he asked you if you were free on Monday.
- Je lui avais dit que c’était urgent. | Say you had told him it was urgent.
- Je te l’envoie ce soir. | Say you’ll send it to them tonight.
- Il y a eu un problème avec la livraison. | Say there has been a problem with the delivery.
