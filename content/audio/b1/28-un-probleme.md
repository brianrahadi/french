## Scene

Your friend Lucas sees that you look stressed. You tell him what happened this morning. This lesson mixes the two past tenses: one for the background, one for what happened.

## Dialogue

- Lucas: Ça va ? Tu as l’air stressé. | Are you OK? You look stressed.
- Alex: Pas vraiment. J’ai perdu mon portefeuille ce matin. | Not really. I lost my wallet this morning.
- Lucas: Oh non ! Comment c’est arrivé ? | Oh no! How did it happen?
- Alex: J’étais dans le métro. Il y avait beaucoup de monde. | I was in the metro. There were lots of people.
- Alex: Quand je suis sorti, tout à coup, je ne l’avais plus. | When I got out, suddenly, I didn’t have it any more.
- Lucas: Qu’est-ce que tu as fait ? | What did you do?
- Alex: Je ne savais pas quoi faire. Alors, je suis allé au commissariat. | I didn’t know what to do. So I went to the police station.
- Lucas: Et alors ? | And then?
- Alex: Heureusement, quelqu’un l’avait rapporté ! | Luckily, someone had handed it in!

## Phrases

- Tu as l’air · stressé | You look stressed
- J’ai perdu · mon portefeuille | I lost my wallet
- Comment · c’est arrivé ? | How did it happen?
- J’étais · dans le métro | I was in the metro | This tense paints the background: where you were, what it was like.
- Il y avait · beaucoup de monde | There were lots of people
- tout à coup | suddenly | After this, the other past tense: the event that happened.
- Je ne savais pas · quoi faire | I didn’t know what to do
- alors | so
- Je suis allé · au commissariat | I went to the police station
- heureusement | luckily

## Practice

- J’étais dans le train, et tout à coup, j’ai perdu mon téléphone. | Say you were on the train, and suddenly, you lost your phone.
- Il y avait beaucoup de monde dans le métro. | Say there were lots of people in the metro.
- Je ne savais pas quoi faire, alors je suis rentré. | Say you didn’t know what to do, so you went home.
- Heureusement, ce n’est pas grave. | Say that luckily, it doesn’t matter.
- Comment c’est arrivé ? Tu étais où ? | Ask how it happened, and where your friend was.
