## Scene

Tuesday morning at work. Your colleague Nadia asks what you did yesterday. You’ll learn to talk about the past with the most common past tense.

## Dialogue

- Nadia: Salut Alex ! Qu’est-ce que tu as fait hier ? | Hi Alex! What did you do yesterday?
- Alex: J’ai visité un musée avec des amis. | I visited a museum with some friends.
- Nadia: Ah oui ? Tu as aimé ? | Oh yes? Did you like it?
- Alex: Oui, c’était super. Et après, on a mangé une pizza. | Yes, it was great. And afterwards we ate a pizza.
- Nadia: Moi, j’ai fait le ménage. Toute la journée ! | I did the housework. All day!
- Alex: Tu as fini ? | Did you finish?
- Nadia: Non, pas encore. Mais j’ai acheté un nouvel aspirateur ! | No, not yet. But I bought a new vacuum cleaner!
- Alex: Tu as bien fait ! | Good for you!

## Phrases

- Qu’est-ce que · tu as fait · hier ? | What did you do yesterday?
- J’ai visité · un musée | I visited a museum | The past is built with “I have” plus the verb: I have visited.
- avec des amis | with some friends
- Tu as aimé ? | Did you like it?
- C’était super | It was great
- On a mangé · une pizza | We ate a pizza
- J’ai fait le ménage | I did the housework
- Tu as fini ? | Did you finish?
- pas encore | not yet
- J’ai acheté | I bought
- Tu as bien fait ! | Good for you! | Literally, “you did well”.

## Practice

- Hier, j’ai regardé un film. | Say that yesterday you watched a film.
- On a mangé au restaurant. | Say that you and your friends ate at a restaurant.
- Tu as travaillé hier ? | Ask a friend if they worked yesterday.
- Non, je n’ai pas fini. | Say no, you didn’t finish.
- J’ai acheté un livre ce matin. | Say you bought a book this morning.
