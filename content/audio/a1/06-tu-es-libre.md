## Scene

Your French friend Hugo calls you. He’d like to do something this evening. Friends speak more casually with each other, so listen to how the questions change.

## Dialogue

- Hugo: Salut Alex ! Tu es libre ce soir ? | Hi Alex! Are you free tonight?
- Alex: Oui, pourquoi ? | Yes, why?
- Hugo: Tu veux aller au cinéma avec moi ? | Do you want to go to the cinema with me?
- Alex: Bonne idée ! À quelle heure ? | Good idea! What time?
- Hugo: À huit heures, devant le cinéma. | At eight, in front of the cinema.
- Alex: D’accord. Et après, on mange ensemble ? | OK. And afterwards, shall we eat together?
- Hugo: Avec plaisir ! À ce soir ! | With pleasure! See you tonight!
- Alex: À ce soir ! | See you tonight!

## Phrases

- salut | hi | Only with friends and family. With strangers, keep the formal hello.
- Tu es libre · ce soir ? | Are you free tonight? | This uses the informal word for “you”, the one for friends and family.
- pourquoi ? | why?
- Tu veux · aller au cinéma ? | Do you want to go to the cinema?
- avec moi | with me
- Bonne idée ! | Good idea!
- d’accord | OK
- On mange · ensemble ? | Shall we eat together? | In everyday speech, French uses a little word meaning “one” to say “we”.
- avec plaisir | with pleasure
- À ce soir ! | See you tonight!

## Practice

- Tu veux manger avec moi ce soir ? | Ask a friend if they want to eat with you tonight.
- D’accord, à huit heures. | Say OK, at eight.
- Tu es libre demain ? | Ask a friend if they’re free tomorrow.
- Bonne idée ! À quelle heure ? | Say good idea, and ask what time.
- Avec plaisir ! À ce soir ! | Accept with pleasure, and say see you tonight.
