## Scene

Your colleague Inès is organising a dinner at her place. She invites you, but you already have plans. You’ll learn to say no politely, give a reason and suggest another day.

## Dialogue

- Inès: Alex, je fais un dîner samedi soir. Tu veux venir ? | Alex, I’m having a dinner on Saturday night. Do you want to come?
- Alex: C’est gentil, mais je ne peux pas. Je vais à un mariage. | That’s kind, but I can’t. I’m going to a wedding.
- Inès: Oh, dommage ! | Oh, what a shame!
- Alex: Je suis vraiment désolé. Tu es libre dimanche ? | I’m really sorry. Are you free on Sunday?
- Inès: Dimanche midi, oui. | Sunday lunchtime, yes.
- Alex: Alors je t’invite au restaurant. Ça te dit ? | Then I’ll take you out to a restaurant. How does that sound?
- Inès: Avec plaisir ! On se retrouve où ? | I’d love to! Where shall we meet?
- Alex: Devant la gare, à midi et demi. Je t’envoie l’adresse. | In front of the station, at half past twelve. I’ll send you the address.

## Phrases

- Tu veux venir ? | Do you want to come?
- C’est gentil · mais je ne peux pas | That’s kind, but I can’t | Start with thanks before you say no. It softens the refusal.
- Je vais · à un mariage | I’m going to a wedding
- Dommage ! | What a shame!
- Je suis · vraiment désolé | I’m really sorry
- Tu es libre · dimanche ? | Are you free on Sunday?
- Je t’invite · au restaurant | I’ll take you out to a restaurant | Here, inviting someone usually means you pay.
- Ça te dit ? | How does that sound? | Very common between friends. Literally, “does it speak to you?”
- On se retrouve · où ? | Where shall we meet?
- Je t’envoie · l’adresse | I’ll send you the address

## Practice

- C’est gentil, mais je ne peux pas. Je travaille. | Thank them, but say you can’t because you’re working.
- Tu es libre vendredi soir ? | Ask if they’re free on Friday evening.
- Ça te dit d’aller au cinéma ? | Ask how going to the cinema sounds.
- On se retrouve devant le musée ? | Suggest meeting in front of the museum.
- Je t’envoie un message demain. | Say you’ll send them a message tomorrow.
