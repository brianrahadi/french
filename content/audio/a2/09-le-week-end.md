## Scene

It’s Monday morning. Your colleague Camille asks about your weekend. You went to the seaside; she stayed at home. You’ll talk about the past for the first time.

## Dialogue

- Camille: Salut Alex ! Tu as passé un bon week-end ? | Hi Alex! Did you have a good weekend?
- Alex: Oui, super ! Je suis allé à la mer. | Yes, great! I went to the seaside.
- Camille: Ah bon ? Qu’est-ce que tu as fait ? | Oh really? What did you do?
- Alex: J’ai mangé des fruits de mer et j’ai beaucoup dormi. | I ate seafood and I slept a lot.
- Camille: Il a fait beau ? | Was the weather nice?
- Alex: Oui, il a fait très beau. Et toi ? | Yes, the weather was lovely. And you?
- Camille: Moi, je suis restée à la maison. J’ai regardé des films. | I stayed at home. I watched films.
- Alex: C’était bien ? | Was it good?
- Camille: Oui, c’était parfait pour se reposer. | Yes, it was perfect for resting.

## Phrases

- Tu as passé · un bon week-end ? | Did you have a good weekend?
- Je suis allé · à la mer | I went to the seaside | For going somewhere, the past is built with the verb “to be”, not “to have”.
- Qu’est-ce que · tu as fait ? | What did you do?
- J’ai mangé | I ate
- J’ai beaucoup dormi | I slept a lot
- Il a fait beau ? | Was the weather nice?
- Je suis resté · à la maison | I stayed at home
- J’ai regardé · des films | I watched films
- C’était bien ? | Was it good?
- Et toi ? | And you? | The informal version, for friends and colleagues.

## Practice

- Je suis allé au cinéma. | Say you went to the cinema.
- J’ai mangé au restaurant. | Say you ate at a restaurant.
- Tu as regardé un film ? | Ask a friend if they watched a film.
- Il a fait beau, c’était parfait. | Say the weather was nice, it was perfect.
- Je suis resté à la maison et j’ai beaucoup dormi. | Say you stayed at home and slept a lot.
