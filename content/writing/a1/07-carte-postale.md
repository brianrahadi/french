You are on holiday. Write a postcard to a friend: where you are, what the weather is like, what there is to see, and one thing you like.

## Phrases

- Je suis à… depuis…
- Il fait beau / Il pleut…
- Il y a une belle plage et…
- Le matin, je… L’après-midi, je…
- Bises, …
