Tell a friend about a real conversation you had recently (with a colleague, a neighbour, someone in your family). Report what each person said, asked and suggested, without quoting directly.

## Phrases

- Il m’a dit qu’il…
- Elle m’a demandé si…
- Je lui ai répondu que…
- Il voulait savoir ce que…
- Finalement, elle m’a conseillé de…
