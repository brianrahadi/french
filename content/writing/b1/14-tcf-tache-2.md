TCF practice, task 2. A website for newcomers asks readers to share their experiences. Write an article about your first weeks in a new country, city or job: what happened, what surprised you, what was difficult, and what advice you would give to someone in the same situation.

## Phrases

- Quand je suis arrivé(e) à…, je ne connaissais personne.
- Ce qui m’a le plus surpris, c’est…
- Au début, j’avais du mal à…
- Heureusement, j’ai rencontré…
- Si je devais donner un conseil, ce serait de…
